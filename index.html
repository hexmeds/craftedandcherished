Hello there!

I’m Stephanie

I’m the maker, designer, and creative heart behind Crafted & Cherished. What began as a love for yarn and quiet moments of stitching has grown into a space where I share crochet patterns, tutorials, and handmade inspiration for every season of life.

Whether I’m designing a cozy wearable, experimenting with new stitches, or simplifying a pattern for beginners, my goal is always the same: to help you create something beautiful with confidence and joy.

I believe crochet is more than a craft — it’s a way to slow down, connect, and turn simple materials into something meaningful. Here, you’ll find projects made with care, encouragement for every skill level, and inspiration to stitch at your own pace.
