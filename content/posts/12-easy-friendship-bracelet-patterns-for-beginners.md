Friendship bracelets are a timeless craft. Whether you remember making them at summer camp or you're just now discovering the joys of knotting, there is something incredibly special about creating a wearable piece of art for someone you care about.

If you are just starting out, the world of embroidery floss and knotting techniques can seem a little overwhelming. But don't worry! We have rounded up the absolute best **easy friendship bracelet patterns for beginners**. These 12 designs require no prior experience, use basic knots, and will have you churning out beautiful, stackable bracelets in no time.

### 1\. Candy Stripe Friendship Bracelets

 

![](https://craftedandcherished.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/1-1.webp)

The ultimate rite of passage for any crafter! The Candy Stripe is arguably the most famous of all friendship bracelet designs. It relies on simple, repetitive forward and backward knots to create bold, colorful stripes. It’s the perfect starting point to get your fingers used to the tension and rhythm of bracelet making.

**Get the tutorial here:** [Candy Stripe Friendship Bracelets by The Neon Tea Party](https://theneonteaparty.com/online-craft-studio/friendship-bracelets/how-to-candy-stripe-friendship-bracelets/)

### 2\. Chinese Staircase / Ladder Friendship Bracelet

 

![](https://craftedandcherished.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/2-1.webp)

If you want a pattern that works up incredibly fast, the Chinese Staircase (also known as the Ladder) is it. Instead of knotting horizontally across multiple strings, you use one string to knot around all the others in a continuous column, creating a cool, twisted spiral effect. It’s visually striking but surprisingly simple to master.

**Get the tutorial here:** [Chinese Staircase Tutorial by Design Dazzle](https://www.designdazzle.com/chinese-ladder-friendship-bracelet-idea-tutorial/)

### 3\. Zig Zag Friendship Bracelet Pattern with a 3D effect!

 

![](https://craftedandcherished.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/3-1.webp)

Take your knotting to the next dimension—literally! This zig-zag pattern uses a clever knotting technique that makes the colored zig-zags actually pop off the base of the bracelet, giving it a unique, textured 3D effect. Don't let the raised design intimidate you; the step-by-step guide breaks it down perfectly for novices.

  * **Get the tutorial here:** [3D Zig Zag Bracelet by Moms and Crafters](https://www.momsandcrafters.com/zig-zag-friendship-bracelet/)



### 4\. Three Strand Braid

 

![](https://craftedandcherished.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/4-1.webp)

Sometimes, going back to absolute basics is exactly what you need. The classic three-strand braid is an essential skill for any jewelry maker. It’s a fantastic, zero-stress pattern for absolute beginners. To make it look like a traditional friendship bracelet, simply use multiple strands of embroidery floss grouped together for a chunky, colorful look.

  * **Get the tutorial here:** [Three Strand Braid by Animated Knots](https://www.animatedknots.com/three-strand-braid-knot)



### 5\. Chain Link Pattern

![Image for Chain Link Pattern](https://www.sixcleversisters.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/03/image_preview.jpg)

For a delicate, modern look, try the Chain Link pattern. This design creates a series of interconnected loops that resemble an actual metal chain link. It introduces beginners to a different type of knot tension and is a great break from standard flat stripe patterns, adding wonderful variety to your bracelet stack.

  * **Get the pattern here:** [Chain Link Pattern on BraceletBook](https://www.braceletbook.com/patterns/normal/28403/)



### 6\. Arrowhead Diamond Pattern

 

![](https://craftedandcherished.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/6-1.webp)

Geometric patterns are incredibly popular, and the Arrowhead Diamond is a stunning entry point into this style. It creates a series of V-shapes that form beautiful diamond motifs down the length of the bracelet. It’s a fantastic beginner pattern for practicing the transition between forward-backward and backward-forward knots.

  * **Get the pattern here:** [Arrowhead Diamond Pattern on BraceletBook](https://www.braceletbook.com/patterns/normal/63/)



### 7\. Daisy Chain Bracelet

 

![](https://craftedandcherished.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/7-1.webp)

Bring a touch of nature to your wrist with the Daisy Chain bracelet. This sweet, floral-inspired pattern uses clever knot placement to create little "petals" along the length of the string. It’s a charming, boho-chic design that looks complex but is totally achievable for a beginner willing to follow a simple grid.

  * **Get the pattern here:** [Daisy Chain Pattern on BraceletBook](https://www.braceletbook.com/patterns/normal/76616/)



### 8\. Watermelon Bracelet Pattern

 

![](https://craftedandcherished.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/8.webp)

What’s more fun than a themed bracelet? This Watermelon pattern is not only adorable but serves as an excellent lesson in color blocking and pattern reading. By alternating greens, whites, and pinks/red, you’ll create a striped design that perfectly mimics everyone's favorite summer fruit.

  * **Get the pattern here:** [Watermelon Pattern on BraceletBook](https://www.braceletbook.com/patterns/normal/35324/)



### 9\. Heart Friendship Bracelet

 

![](https://craftedandcherished.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/9.webp)

Show your friends how much you care by weaving them a Heart Friendship Bracelet. This pattern introduces beginners to the concept of "shaping" within a knotting grid—creating curves and points using straight knots. It’s a timeless, love-filled design that makes the perfect handmade gift for birthdays or Valentine's Day.

  * **Get the pattern here:** [Heart Pattern on BraceletBook](https://www.braceletbook.com/patterns/normal/150/)



### 10\. Zipper Bracelet

 

![](https://craftedandcherished.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/10.webp)

The Zipper bracelet gets its name from its tight, interlocking appearance that perfectly resembles a closed jacket zipper. This pattern is excellent for beginners because it heavily focuses on symmetry and rhythm. It will teach you how to keep your edges clean and your knots tight, which is a crucial skill for advancing your craft.

  * **Get the pattern here:** [Zipper Pattern on BraceletBook](https://www.braceletbook.com/patterns/normal/3982/)



### 11\. Diagonal Zig Zag Pattern

 

![](https://craftedandcherished.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/11.webp)

Unlike the 3D zig-zag mentioned earlier in this list, this Diagonal Zig Zag lays completely flat, offering a sleek, woven aesthetic. It’s a wonderful beginner pattern for learning how to smoothly transition your knots from the left side of the bracelet to the right side to create continuous, flowing diagonal lines.

  * **Get the pattern here:** [Diagonal Zig Zag Pattern on BraceletBook](https://www.braceletbook.com/patterns/normal/155910/)



### 12\. Alternating Color Links

 

![](https://craftedandcherished.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/12.webp)

If you love highly colorful, blocky designs, the Alternating Color Links pattern is a great choice. It creates a segmented, pixelated look that is very visually satisfying. This is a brilliant beginner project because the repeating color blocks make it very easy to track your progress and spot any mistakes before you knot too far ahead.

  * **Get the pattern here:** [Alternating Color Links on BraceletBook](https://www.braceletbook.com/patterns/normal/83338/)



### Ready, Set, Knot!

The secret to mastering **easy friendship bracelet patterns for beginners** is simply to start! Don't worry if your first few attempts are a little uneven; knotting is a muscle memory skill that improves with every single bracelet you make. Pick out your favorite colors, choose a pattern from the list above, and enjoy the relaxing, screen-free process of creating something beautiful.
