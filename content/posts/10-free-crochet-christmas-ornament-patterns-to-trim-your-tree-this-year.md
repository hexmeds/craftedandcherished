There's something special about a tree decorated with handmade ornaments — each one a little keepsake that gets pulled out year after year. If you're searching for **crochet Christmas ornament patterns** to fill out your tree, this roundup gathers ten free designs, from classic baubles to festive stockings, mittens, and gift boxes. Every pattern below works up quickly with basic stitches and scrap yarn, so grab your hook and let's get started on this year's ornament collection.

 

 

## 1\. Boho Christmas Crochet Baubles

![boho crochet baubles free pattern](https://www.anniedesigncrochet.com/wp-content/uploads/2020/11/crochet-frilly-bauble-9.jpg)

These Boho Christmas Crochet Baubles use vibrant folk colors or traditional Christmas shades to create bright, textured ball ornaments that slip right over plain baubles from the store. The design uses small amounts of cotton yarn, making it an easy stash-buster for anyone who loves a colorful, handmade tree. [See the full Boho Christmas Baubles pattern here](https://www.anniedesigncrochet.com/boho-christmas-crochet-baubles-free-pattern/) to start stitching your own set.

## 2\. How to Crochet a Christmas Ornament for Beginners

![](https://www.sigonimacaroni.com/wp-content/uploads/2021/12/IMG_8734-1024x683.jpg)

This beginner-friendly tutorial teaches you to crochet a bauble cover that fits directly over an 80mm plastic craft ornament, so you get a clean, sturdy shape without any guesswork. It's an easy first ornament project if you're new to crochet and want something that works up in an evening. [Follow the full beginner Christmas ornament tutorial](https://www.sigonimacaroni.com/how-to-crochet-a-christmas-ornament-for-beginners/) to make your first handmade bauble.

## 3\. Christmas Traditions Ornament

![a crochet Christmas Ornament](https://richtexturescrochet.com/wp-content/uploads/2021/12/182D70FB-213D-4654-A7AF-92D42A2FC829-1024x683.jpeg)

The Christmas Traditions Ornament is a crocheted granny circle attached to a metal macramé ring, making it a lovely way to use up small amounts of light-weight yarn scraps from your stash. Its simple construction and colorful rounds make it a satisfying one-hour project. [Check out the full Christmas Traditions Ornament pattern](https://richtexturescrochet.com/christmas-traditions-ornament-crochet-pattern/) for the complete step-by-step.

## 4\. Festive Trees Fair Isle Bauble

![](https://www.hanjancrochet.com/wp-content/uploads/2022/11/day-2-dec-6-festive-trees-ig-cover.jpg)

 

For a more advanced colorwork project, the Festive Trees bauble uses the Fair Isle crochet technique to stitch two mini Christmas trees right into the fabric of the ornament. It's a great way to build your colorwork skills while making something genuinely festive for the tree. [See the full Festive Trees Fair Isle Bauble pattern](https://www.hanjancrochet.com/festive-tree-crochet-bauble-pattern/) to try this fun technique yourself.

## 5\. Beads and Baubles Ornaments

![Beaded crochet ornaments hung on a tree branch.](https://makeanddocrew.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/12/beaded-crochet-ornaments.jpg)

 

This clever pattern lets you add rows of beads directly into your stitches as you crochet, so there's no need to pre-string anything before you start. Worked over plastic fillable ornaments, it's an easy way to add extra sparkle and texture to your tree. [Get the full Beads and Baubles Ornaments pattern](https://makeanddocrew.com/crochet-christmas-ornaments-beads/) to try this beaded technique.

## 6\. Mini Crochet Stockings

![Three miniature crochet stockings hanging from a mantel with decorations.](https://i0.wp.com/www.jenhayescreations.com/wp-content/uploads/2019/11/Stocking_Mini_2019_01.jpg?resize=800%2C533&ssl=1)

These Mini Crochet Stockings come in six adorable variations, worked from the toe up with a contrasting heel, so you can make a whole matching (or mismatched) set for the tree or mantel. They're roomy enough to tuck in a small treat or trinket, making them as functional as they are cute. [Follow the full Mini Crochet Stockings pattern](https://www.jenhayescreations.com/mini-crochet-stockings/) to make your own set.

## 7\. Paw Print Christmas Tree Ornament

![](https://www.simplyhookedbyjanet.com/wp-content/uploads/2019/10/paw.jpegfit6402c640ssl1.jpg)

Perfect for any dog or cat lover, this Paw Print ornament is a quick, easy applique-style pattern that makes a heartfelt gift for pet owners on your list. It's simple enough to whip up several in an evening, in colors to match your own pets or your Christmas theme. [Check out the full Paw Print Ornament pattern](https://www.simplyhookedbyjanet.com/paw-print-christmas-tree-ornament-crochet-pattern/) to make one for every animal lover you know.

## 8\. Crochet Christmas Tree Ornament (Corner-to-Corner)

![](https://i2.wp.com/www.thepurpleponcho.com/wp-content/uploads/2019/12/Crochet-Christmas-Tree-Ornament-Bunting-on-thepurpleponcho.com-Crochet-by-Carolyn.jpg?fit=607%2C1024&ssl=1)

Worked in the popular corner-to-corner (C2C) technique, this little Christmas tree ornament comes together fast and uses only a minimal amount of yarn, making it a great scrap-buster project. Dress it up with beads or metallic thread for a bit of extra tinsel-inspired sparkle. [See the full C2C Christmas Tree Ornament pattern](https://www.thepurpleponcho.com/crochet-christmas-tree-ornament/) for the complete tutorial.

## 9\. Pretty Present Ornament

![two crochet ornaments on a fur background with text overlay "pretty present ornament - free crochet pattern from you should craft"](https://i0.wp.com/www.youshouldcraft.com/wp-content/uploads/2021/10/present-crochet-ornament-pinterest1.jpg?resize=1000%2C1500&ssl=1)

This Pretty Present Ornament pattern features two stuffed gift-box shapes — square and rectangular — built with nothing but single crochet stitches, making it an ideal project for beginners. Tie on a bit of ribbon and it doubles as a sweet gift topper as well as a tree ornament. [Follow the full Pretty Present Ornament pattern](https://www.youshouldcraft.com/present-crochet-ornament-pattern/) to make your own mini gift boxes.

## 10\. Mini Mittens Ornament

![Mini Mittens Christmas crochet ornament free pattern with video tutorial](https://littlecrochetfarm.com/wp-content/uploads/2023/11/Mini-Mittens-Christmas-crochet-ornament-free-pattern-0008.jpg)

These tiny crocheted mittens make an adorable addition to any tree, and their small size means they're a great way to use up leftover scraps of sport or cotton-weight yarn in festive colors. They also work well as gift tags or garland pieces if you want to spread the mitten cheer beyond the tree. [Get the full Mini Mittens Ornament pattern](https://littlecrochetfarm.com/mini-mittens-ornament-free-crochet-pattern/) to start stitching this sweet little design.

## Final Thoughts

From colorwork baubles to beaded ornaments, tiny stockings, and pet-themed keepsakes, these ten crochet Christmas ornament patterns offer plenty of variety for decorating your tree this year. Pick a few that fit your skill level and yarn stash, and you'll have a handmade, one-of-a-kind collection to hang up for holidays to come.
