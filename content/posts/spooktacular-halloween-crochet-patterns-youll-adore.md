## Common Questions

**How hard are Halloween crochet patterns?**  
Oh, you don't need wizard skills! Most patterns are newbie-friendly--think single and double crochet, mostly.

**What yarn should I use?**  
Acrylic yarn works best for durability (especially if little hands will tug on them). Go wild with colors!

**Can I crochet these with leftover yarn?**  
Absolutely. Some of the cutest projects are yarn-scrap miracles. Speaking as someone who never throws anything away!

**Where can I find free patterns?**  
Check Pinterest, crochet blogs, and, yeah, Ravelry. Or just peek at the [free baby blanket crochet patterns](https://craftedandcherished.com/cozy-baby-blanket-crochet-patterns-free/) for inspiration.
