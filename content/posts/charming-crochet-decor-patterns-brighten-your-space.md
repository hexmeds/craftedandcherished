## Tips for Beginners in Crochet

Okay, pause--if you're new to crocheting, don't let those intricate patterns freak you out. Been there, unraveled that!

  * Start with simple squares: Make a dishcloth first instead of a pillowcase.
  * Use chunkier yarn: It's honestly way easier to handle.
  * YouTube is your pal: If you get tangled up (happens to the best of us), hit up a video tutorial.
  * Don't stress perfection: Even the wobbly ones look charming--call it "character!"



The struggle bus is temporary, the satisfaction is forever.
