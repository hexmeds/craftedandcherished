# Summer Crochet Tee Top Pattern: A Stylish Addition to Your Wardrobe

As the warm season approaches, there’s nothing quite like a handmade crochet tee to elevate your summer wardrobe. This article will guide you through a delightful crochet tee top pattern, allowing you to create a stylish and comfortable garment that reflects your personal style. With easy-to-follow instructions, this pattern is perfect for crocheters of all skill levels. So grab your hooks and let’s dive into the world of summer crochet!

## Why Crocheting Your Own Tee Top is a Great Idea

Crocheting your own clothes comes with a plethora of benefits. Here are a few reasons why you should consider making your own summer tee:

  1. **Unique Style** : When you crochet your own clothes, you can select your own yarn colors and patterns, ensuring that your tee is one-of-a-kind.

  2. **Comfort and Fit** : Handmade garments can be customized to fit your body perfectly, unlike mass-produced items.

  3. **Quality Materials** : With the right yarn, you can make a tee that is both breathable and comfortable for hot summer days.

  4. **Creative Outlet** : Crocheting is a fantastic way to express your creativity and unwind, all while creating something beautiful.




Now that we’ve highlighted the benefits, let’s get started on your very own summer crochet tee top. Whether you’re hitting the beach or enjoying a picnic in the park, this tee will be your go-to garment for those warm sunny days.

## Materials You Will Need

Before you start crocheting, gather the necessary materials:

  * **Yarn** : Choose a lightweight cotton or bamboo yarn, which is breathable and perfect for summer. Depending on your size, you will need approximately 300-600 yards of yarn.

  * **Crochet Hook** : An H/8 (5.00 mm) crochet hook is recommended, but check your yarn label for the best size.

  * **Scissors** : A pair of scissors for cutting your yarn.

  * **Tapestry Needle** : For weaving in ends and sewing pieces together.

  * **Measuring Tape** : To ensure you achieve the perfect fit.

  * **Stitch Markers** : To keep track of your stitches and rows.




## Stitches Used

Familiarize yourself with the following stitches used in this pattern:

  * **Chain (ch)** : The foundation of your project.

  * **Single Crochet (sc)** : A basic stitch that provides a tight fabric that's perfect for the body of your tee.

  * **Double Crochet (dc)** : A taller stitch that adds an airy feel to your tee.

  * **Slip Stitch (sl st)** : Used to join rounds or pieces together.




If you are new to crocheting, it might be helpful to look up tutorials on these stitches to ensure you understand the basics.

## Summer Tee Top Pattern Instructions

Now that you have all your materials, let's get to the fun part—actually making the top! This pattern is worked from the bottom up and consists of two main pieces: the front and back.

### Step 1: Foundation Chain

Start by making a foundation chain. To determine the length of your chain, measure your bust circumference and divide by 4. Then, add an additional 5” for ease. This will give you the number of chains you need to create for the bottom of your tee.

**Example** : If your bust is 36”, your foundation chain would be 36 ÷ 4 + 5 = 14 + 5 = **19 chains**.

### Step 2: Row 1

Once you have your foundation chain, turn your work and do the following:

  * **Row 1** : Starting from the second chain from the hook, sc across (you should have 18 sc total if you followed the example).



### Step 3: Establishing the Pattern

From here, you will establish a pattern using a combination of single crochet and double crochet stitches.

### Step 4: Continue Rows

  * **Row 2** : Ch 2 (this counts as your first dc), dc in the next stitch, *sk 1 st, dc in the next st; repeat from * across to the end, finish with a sc in the last st.

  * **Repeat** : Continue alternating between Rows 1 and 2 until your piece measures approximately 20 inches or your desired length.




### Step 5: Shaping

Once you reach the desired length, it’s time to shape the armholes. For this, you will create a slightly longer section on the back part of the top.

### Step 6: Create the Back

  * For the back piece, repeat Steps 1 to 5. However, make the back approximately 3 inches longer than the front for added comfort and style.



### Step 7: Joining the Pieces

Once you have both the front and back pieces, it’s time to join them.

  * Align the front and back pieces with the right sides facing each other.

  * Using slip stitches or a whip stitch, sew the shoulder seams together, leaving about 5-6 inches for armholes on each side.




### Step 8: Finishing Touches

After you’ve joined the shoulder seams, it’s time to add some finishing touches:

  * **Armholes** : Crochet a few rounds of sc around the armholes to clean up the edges and provide a nice finish.

  * **Neckline** : You may want to do the same around the neckline to create a polished look.




### Step 9: Weaving in Ends

With your crochet tee top nearly complete, make sure to use your tapestry needle to weave in any loose ends.

### Step 10: Block Your Tee

To give your crochet tee top a beautiful finish, consider blocking your piece. This can be done by lightly steaming the fabric or soaking it in cool water and laying it flat to dry. This step helps even out your stitches and gives the top a shop-quality look.

## Styling Your Summer Crochet Tee

The great part about a crochet tee is its versatility. Here are a few styling tips to make the most out of your beautiful handmade piece:

  1. **Casual Look** : Pair your crochet tee with your favorite denim shorts and sandals for a laid-back summer vibe.

  2. **Chic Ensemble** : Tuck your tee into a flowy maxi skirt and accessorize with statement jewelry for a more polished look.

  3. **Layering** : Use your crochet tee as a layering piece over a tank top or dress. Add a light cardigan or kin to stay cozy on cooler summer evenings.

  4. **Accessorizing** : Hats, sunglasses, and beach bags make excellent accessories to complete your summer outfit.
