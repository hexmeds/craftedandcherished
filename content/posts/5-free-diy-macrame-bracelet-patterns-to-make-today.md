Macrame jewelry is having a major moment, and there is no better way to get in on the trend than by making your own wearable art. Whether you are looking for a relaxing weekend hobby, a thoughtful handmade gift, or new inventory to sell at craft fairs, knotting your own bracelets is incredibly rewarding.

If you are ready to dive into the world of knotting, we have rounded up the best **free DIY macrame bracelet patterns** available online. From beachy cowrie shells to retro hemp styles, these five designs are fun, fast, and totally customizable to your personal style.

### 1\. Easy Beaded Macrame Bracelet (with Sliding Closure!)

![finished beaded macrame bracelets](https://www.marchingnorth.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/03/beaded-macrame-bracelets_0049_Beaded-Macrame-Bracelets.00_12_27_19.Still001.jpg.jpg)

Looking for a simple but beautiful macrame bracelet pattern? We’ve got the perfect one for you! This beaded macrame bracelet uses waxed 1 mm cord and some lovely beads for a quick and easy jewelry pattern. Because the waxed cord holds its shape so well, the final result looks incredibly professional.

It’s a fun project that works up quickly—you can easily have a few of these done in a single afternoon. You can make them for yourself, gift them to friends, or even batch-produce them to sell at your next craft fair. Plus, the sliding closure means you don't have to fuss with difficult metal clasps!

#### **Get the free pattern here:** [Easy Beaded Macrame Bracelet by Marching North](https://www.marchingnorth.com/free-macrame-bracelet-patterns/)

### 2\. Beachy Cowrie Shell Macrame Bracelet

![finished cowrie shell macrame bracelet](https://www.marchingnorth.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/04/diy-macrame-cowrie-shell-bracelet_0032_Screenshot-2026-04-22-at-3.37.12-PM.png.jpg)

Channel those warm summer memories and get that perfect beachy vibe with this simple cowrie shell bracelet pattern! Coastal aesthetics remain incredibly popular in jewelry design, and this pattern captures that effortlessly cool look.

These bracelets work up so fast, making them the ideal last-minute accessory before a vacation. Better yet, they are a fantastic, creative way to finally use those cowrie shells you’ve had sitting in your craft stash forever. Pair the shells with some neutral cotton cord for a timeless, ocean-inspired look.

#### **Get the free pattern here:** [Beachy Cowrie Shell Bracelet by Marching North](https://www.marchingnorth.com/diy-cowrie-shell-macrame-bracelet/)

### 3\. 3 Simple Macrame Hemp Bracelet Patterns

![3 hemp bracelets on a woman's wrist](https://www.marchingnorth.com/wp-content/uploads/2021/03/hemp-macrame-bracelets-1.jpg)

Nostalgia is a huge trend right now, and this macrame bracelet tutorial gives off all the best 90’s and Y2K vibes. If you’re looking for a more masculine, natural style, these hemp bracelets are absolutely perfect!

You only need to know a few basic knots and grab some natural hemp cord, which makes these cool macrame bracelets super beginner-friendly. The best part? The sizing is completely foolproof. The length can be easily customized to your desired fit simply by adding more knots or tying less—easy peasy!

#### **Get the free pattern here:** [Simple Hemp Bracelets by Marching North](https://www.marchingnorth.com/macrame-hemp-bracelet-patterns/)

Friendship bracelets never truly go out of style. This collection of 6 easy patterns is a fantastic way to entertain some crafty teens on a rainy day, but adults love them just as much!

### 5\. 2 Color Sliding Square Knot Bracelets

![featured image for 2 color reversible macrame bracelet tutorial](https://www.marchingnorth.com/wp-content/uploads/2023/01/2-color-macrame-bracelet-featured-image21.jpg)![finished bulky cotton square knot bracelet](https://www.marchingnorth.com/wp-content/uploads/2023/01/how-to-make-a-2-color-square-knot-bracelet_0041_2-Color-Macrame-Bracelet.00_19_37_13.Still432.jpg-1000x654.jpg)

 

These square knot bracelets are super easy to make, but by using two contrasting colors, it gives the final piece a totally different, elevated look. Color-blocking is a great way to make basic knots look highly designed.

The really cool thing about this specific design is that the bracelets are completely reversible! If you flip them over, they feature a different color pattern on the other side—meaning you are essentially getting two bracelets in one. Not to mention, the sliding knot closure makes taking them on and off infinitely easier. Who knew you could make a simple knot look so pretty?

#### **Get the free pattern here:** [2 Color Reversible Bracelet by Marching North](https://www.marchingnorth.com/2-color-macrame-square-knot-bracelet-reversible/)
