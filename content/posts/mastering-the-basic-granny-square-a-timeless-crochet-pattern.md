The granny square is a crochet classic that has stood the test of time. This iconic motif is beloved by both beginners and experienced crocheters alike for its simplicity, versatility, and endless design possibilities. Whether you're looking to create a cozy blanket, a stylish bag, or a colorful piece of home decor, the basic granny square pattern is the perfect starting point.

## What is a Granny Square?

A granny square is a square-shaped crochet motif typically worked in rounds from the center outward. The classic design features clusters of double crochet stitches separated by chain stitches, with additional chain spaces at each corner to create the square shape.

The beauty of the granny square lies in its adaptability. By using different colors, yarn weights, and hook sizes, you can create an infinite variety of looks. Granny squares can be worked in a single color for a cohesive appearance or in multiple colors for a playful, scrappy effect.

## Why Crochet Granny Squares?

There are many reasons why the granny square continues to be a favorite among crocheters:

  1. **Beginner-friendly:** The basic granny square pattern is easy to learn and memorize, making it an ideal project for crochet newbies. It's a great way to practice basic stitches and get comfortable working in rounds.
  2. **Portable:** Granny squares are small and portable, making them perfect for on-the-go crocheting. You can easily stash a project in your bag and work on a few squares whenever you have a spare moment.
  3. **Stash-busting:** Have a bunch of yarn scraps lying around? Granny squares are an excellent way to use up those odds and ends. Mix and match colors for a unique, eclectic look.
  4. **Versatile:** The humble granny square can be transformed into an endless array of projects, from afghans and throw pillows to sweaters, bags, and more. The only limit is your imagination!
  5. **Mindful crafting:** The repetitive nature of crocheting granny squares can be incredibly meditative and calming. Focusing on each stitch can help quiet the mind and promote a sense of well-being.



## Basic Granny Square Pattern

Ready to get started? Here's a step-by-step guide to crocheting a basic granny square.

### Materials

  * Any weight yarn (cotton, acrylic, or wool all work well)
  * Appropriately sized crochet hook (check out our guide to the [best ergonomic crochet hooks](https://craftedandcherished.com/best-ergonomic-crochet-hooks/) for comfort!)
  * Tapestry needle
  * Scissors



### Crochet Abbreviations

  * ch = chain
  * dc = double crochet
  * sl st = slip stitch



### Instructions

  1. To begin, make a slip knot and chain 5. Join with a slip stitch in the first chain to form a ring.
  2. Round 1: Ch 3 (counts as 1st dc). Work 2 dc, ch 2, 3 dc, ch 2, 3 dc, ch 2, 3 dc, ch 2 into the ring. Join with a sl st to the top of the starting ch 3. (12 dc, 4 ch-2 sps)
  3. Round 2: Sl st into the next ch-2 sp. Ch 3, 2 dc, ch 2, 3 dc in the same sp (1st corner made). *Ch 1, [3 dc, ch 2, 3 dc] in the next ch-2 sp; repeat from * twice more. Ch 1, join with a sl st to the top of the starting ch 3.
  4. Round 3: Sl st into the next ch-2 sp. Ch 3, 2 dc, ch 2, 3 dc in the same sp. *Ch 1, 3 dc in the next ch-1 sp, ch 1, [3 dc, ch 2, 3 dc] in the next ch-2 sp; rep from * twice more. Ch 1, 3 dc in the last ch-1 sp, ch 1, join with a sl st to the top of the starting ch 3.
  5. To work additional rounds, sl st to the next corner space. Work [3 dc, ch 2, 3 dc] in each corner ch-2 sp and 3 dc in each ch-1 sp along the sides, adding a ch-1 between each 3-dc group. Continue in this manner until your granny square reaches the desired size.
