Get ready for the spookiest season of the year with a hauntingly cute addition to your crochet queue! This ghost granny square features a classic white phantom floating against a deep black background, capturing that perfect Halloween vibe with its expressive little face and wavy shape.

Don’t be spooked by the colorwork—this pattern is surprisingly beginner-friendly. Using only basic stitches like single and double crochet, it is a fantastic way to practice color changes and textured shaping on a smaller scale before tackling a massive project.

_This pattern uses USA crochet terminology._

## Materials Needed

  * Yarn of your choice (2 colors)
  * Crochet hook (3.00mm hook used in the sample)
  * Darning needle
  * Scissors



## Abbreviations Used

  * **ch** = chain stitch
  * **dc** = double crochet
  * **sc** = single crochet
  * **st** = stitch
  * **Sl St** = slip stitch for joining for different round
  * **BLO** = back loop only



## Pattern Instructions

**Round 1:** Start with knot, ch5, 1 sc in the back loop of each of the 4th, 3rd, 2nd chains. 2sc In the back loop of the 1st chain, then 2 sc in the front loop of the same stitch. 1 sc in the front loop of the 2nd, 3rd, and 4th chains, sl st to the 5th chain.

**Round 2:** ch, 1sc in the 5th chain, 2sc in the next st, sc in the following 3 sts, 3dc in the following 2 sts, sc, sc, sl st in the next 4 sts.

**Round 3:** Start from the st you made the last sl st in the previous round, sc, 2dc, ch2, sl st to the next st, sc in the following 5 sts, 2dc in the following 4 sts, sc in the following 7 sts, then 3sc, sc dc, 2dc, ch2, sc in the next 4 sts, dc, dc.

**Round 4:** ch1, flip the workpiece, sc in the same st, sl st in the next 6 sts, sc dc in the next st, ch, sl st to the same st, sl st in the next 12 sts. Flip the workpiece, ch3, dc, sc, sl st to the next st, fasten off.

**Round 5:** BLO. Add new yarn in the back loop of the fifth dc from right to left, ch as a standing st, 2sc in the same st, 2sc in the following 3 sts, sc, 3dc, sc, 2sc, sc in the following 23 sts, 3dc, sc, 2sc in the next 3 sts, sl st to the first ch.

**Round 6:** ch sc in the same st, sc, sc, sc dc, 2dc, 2dc, dc, sc in the following 5 sts, dc in the following 6 sts, 2dc, sc in the following 6 sts, 2sc, sc, sc, sc, dc in the next 3 sts, sc in the next 6 sts, dc in the next 3 sts, 2dc, 2dc, sc, sc, sc, sl st to the first sc.

**Round 7:** ch sc in the same st, sc, dc, dc, 3dc, sc in the following 10 sts, dc, dc, 2dc, dc, 2dc, 2dc, sc in the following 9 sts, 2dc, dc in the next 14 sts, 2dc, 2dc, sc in the next 5 sts, sl st to the first ch.

**Round 8:** ch st in the same st, sc in the following 3 sts, 2sc in the following 3 sts, sc in the following 13 sts, dc, 2dc, 2dc, sc in the next 12 sts, 2sc, sc in the next 16 sts, dc, 2dc, 2dc, sc in the next 6 sts, sl st to the first sc.

**Round 9:** ch sc in the same st, sc in the following 7 sts, 2dc, dc in the following 16 sts, 3dc, dc, sc in the next 14 sts, 2sc, 2sc, sc in the next 16 sts, 2sc in the next 4 sts, sc in the following 7 sts, sl st to the first sc, fasten off. _Embroider eyes and mouth._

## Tips

  * **Gauge & Sizing:** The finished ghost granny square measures about 3.74” x 3.74”.
  * **Yarn Weight & Hooks:** You can use any yarn and corresponding hook size you prefer. However, using a smaller hook relative to your yarn weight helps create a tighter fabric, which is ideal for colorwork so the black background doesn't peek through the white stitches.
  * **Color Combinations:** The classic black and white creates the ultimate ghostly contrast. For extra spookiness, try a glow-in-the-dark white yarn! For a whimsical twist, use a light gray ghost on a deep purple background.
  * If you love this spooky theme, make sure to check out the [Free Spade Granny Square Pattern](https://craftedandcherished.com/free-spade-granny-square-pattern/) or the [Moon Granny Square Pattern](https://craftedandcherished.com/moon-granny-square-pattern/) for a mystical night sky vibe.



## FAQ

**What can I make with these squares?** These ghostly squares are incredibly versatile! They are perfect for Halloween home décor like festive blankets, cushion covers, trick-or-treat bags, wall hangings, or even coasters for your Halloween party. If you need help figuring out how to join them together, this tutorial on [How to Create a Seamless Join for Granny Squares](https://craftedandcherished.com/how-to-create-a-seamless-join-for-granny-squares-step-by-step-tutorial/) is incredibly helpful!

**How do I embroider the face?** Using your darning needle and a length of black yarn, simply stitch two small oval or round eyes and a small, jagged or oval mouth directly into the stitches of the white ghost body.

**My edges are looking a little wonky. How can I fix this?** Graphghan-style colorwork can sometimes cause edges to pull or look uneven. Check out these [3 Secrets to Clean Edges](https://craftedandcherished.com/how-to-crochet-a-perfect-granny-square-3-secrets-to-clean-edges-no-visible-join/) to keep your squares looking perfectly square and professional.
