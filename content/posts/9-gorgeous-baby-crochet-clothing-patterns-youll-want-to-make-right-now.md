Crocheted baby clothes have a charm that store-bought items just can't match — cozy textures, thoughtful details, and that unmistakable handmade touch. If you're hunting for fresh **baby crochet patterns** to add to your queue, this roundup covers nine stunning designs, from hexagon cardigans to a full Santa set. Whether you're making a gift for a new arrival or stocking your handmade shop, there's a pattern here to fall in love with.

## 1\. Cosy Hexi Cardi

![](https://craftedandcherished.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/1-2.webp)

The Cosy Hexi Cardi is built from soft hexagon motifs joined together into a warm, textured little cardigan that's as fun to make as it is to wear. The hexagon construction makes it a satisfying project to work on in sections, and the finished look is anything but basic. [See the full Cosy Hexi Cardi pattern here](https://www.babycrochetdesigns.com/cosy-hexi-cardi) to get the complete instructions and materials list.

## 2\. Rainbow Drops Hooded Poncho

 

![](https://craftedandcherished.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/2-2.webp)

Bright, playful, and practical, the Rainbow Drops Hooded Poncho is perfect for layering over an outfit on cooler days. The colorful "drops" detailing gives it a cheerful look that stands out from typical baby outerwear. [Check out the full Rainbow Drops Hooded Poncho pattern](https://www.babycrochetdesigns.com/rainbow-drops-poncho) to start stitching this eye-catching piece.

## 3\. Pebble Dash Hexagon Cardigan

![](https://craftedandcherished.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/3-2.webp)

 

Another gorgeous hexagon-based design, the Pebble Dash Hexagon Cardigan uses a subtly textured stitch pattern to create a speckled, "pebble dash" effect across the panels. It's a great option if you love the hexagon cardigan style but want something with a bit more texture. [Get the full Pebble Dash Hexagon Cardigan pattern](https://www.babycrochetdesigns.com/pebble-dash-hexagon-cardigan) to see exactly how it comes together.

## 4\. Criss Cross Hexagon Cardigan

![](https://craftedandcherished.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/4-2.webp)

The Criss Cross Hexagon Cardigan takes the hexagon motif in a new direction, arranging the panels in a crossing layout for a bold, graphic finish. It's an eye-catching option for makers who already love the hexagon cardigan family and want to try a variation. [Follow the full Criss Cross Hexagon Cardigan pattern](https://www.babycrochetdesigns.com/criss-cross-hexi-cardi) for the full step-by-step build.

## 5\. Vienna Top Crochet Pattern

![](https://craftedandcherished.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/5-2.webp)

 

If cardigans aren't quite what you're after, the Vienna Top offers a simple, elegant crochet top pattern that works beautifully on its own or layered underneath something warmer. Its clean lines make it a versatile piece for any baby wardrobe. [See the full Vienna Top crochet pattern](https://www.babycrochetdesigns.com/vienna-top) to bring this classic design to life.

## 6\. Santa Set

![](https://craftedandcherished.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/6-2.webp)

 

Get ahead of the holiday rush with the Santa Set, a festive crochet outfit perfect for first Christmas photos or holiday gatherings. This set makes an adorable keepsake gift and a fun seasonal project to work on in the months leading up to December. [Check out the full Santa Set pattern](https://www.babycrochetdesigns.com/santa-set) for everything you need to make it.

## 7\. Baby Boxer Set

![](https://craftedandcherished.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/7-2.webp)

 

For a playful photo-prop idea, the Baby Boxer Set is designed to look like tiny boxing gear, complete with all the fun details that make for an unforgettable photo shoot. It's a standout choice if you're after something a little different from the usual cardigan or romper patterns. [Get the full Baby Boxer Set pattern](https://www.babycrochetdesigns.com/boxing-photo-prop-set) to create this memorable photo prop.

## 8\. Mini Pick & Mix Sweater

![](https://craftedandcherished.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/8-1.webp)

 

The Mini Pick & Mix Sweater is a sweet, candy-inspired design that adds a playful pop of color to any baby's wardrobe. Its fun theme makes it a great conversation piece and an especially charming gift for a baby shower. [Follow the full Mini Pick & Mix Sweater pattern](https://www.babycrochetdesigns.com/mini-pick-and-mix-sweater) to start this cheerful project.

## 9\. Cozy Cardigan – Size Newborn

![](https://craftedandcherished.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/9-1.webp)

 

Rounding out the list is the Cozy Cardigan in newborn size, a simple, classic cardigan pattern ideal for welcoming a brand-new baby. Its straightforward construction makes it a great pick for a quick, heartfelt handmade gift. [See the full Cozy Cardigan (Newborn) pattern here](https://www.babycrochetdesigns.com/cozy-cardigan-newborn) to make this timeless little piece.

## Final Thoughts

From textured hexagon cardigans to festive holiday sets and playful photo props, these nine baby crochet patterns offer something for every style, season, and skill level. Pick the design that speaks to you, gather your yarn, and start creating a handmade piece a new baby (and their parents) will treasure for years to come.
