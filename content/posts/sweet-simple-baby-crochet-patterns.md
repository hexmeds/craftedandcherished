## Best Materials and Tools for Baby Crochet

I see you eyeing the cheap plastic hooks at the store--don't do it. (Trust--a few extra bucks gets you cozy hands.)

  * **Soft, washable yarn** is the golden rule. Babies = drool, spit-up, mystery stains…you need the good stuff.
  * Size G or H crochet hooks work for most projects. Metal or ergonomic ones won't make your hand cramp.
  * Stitch markers changed my life. I used to use paper clips, but these are worth grabbing!
  * Have sharp scissors handy. (That fuzzy yarn? Harder to snip than you think.)

## Common Questions

**Q: Do I need expensive yarn for baby crochet patterns?**  
A: Nope! Go for soft and washable. You want comfort, not complication.

**Q: Is it safe to use buttons or beads on baby projects?**  
A: Avoid tiny bits that could be a choking hazard--embroider details instead for safety.

**Q: How long does it take to finish a baby blanket?**  
A: Depends! A tiny blanket is a weekend project, but big ones might take a week--or longer if you binge-watch while working (guilty).

**Q: Can I resize a pattern if it 's too small or big?**  
A: Totally. Add or drop rows--a little math, but nothing wild.

**Q: What if I mess up? Do I have to start over?**  
A: Rarely. Usually, you can "frog" (rip out) a few rows, or fudge it. Homemade is supposed to be human!
