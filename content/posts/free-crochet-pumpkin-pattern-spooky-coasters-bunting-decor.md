Are you looking for the perfect quick and easy Halloween crochet project? You are in the right place! This **free crochet pumpkin pattern** is fantastic for crafters of all skill levels. Whether you want to make a spooky set of coasters for your autumn coffee table or string them together into a festive Halloween bunting, these little pumpkins are guaranteed to add a handmade touch to your holiday decor.

 

Grab your crochet thread, and let’s get started on this fun and beginner-friendly pattern!

 

## Materials and Supplies

To achieve a delicate and sturdy finish, this pattern uses fine crochet cotton. Here is what you will need:

 

  * **Crochet cotton ball** – Size 7 to 10 (2 or 3 colours: main pumpkin, edging, and eyes/mouth)
  * **Crochet hook** – Size 2.5
  * **Scissors**
  * **Embroidery needle** – rounded point
  * **Spray starch** (optional, for stiffening)



 

>  
> 
> **NOTE:** Crochet thread is labelled with a number, like a 3 or a 10, and this refers to the thickness of the thread. The smaller the number, the thicker the thread. For this project, a size 7 to 10 thread will give you the best results.

## Abbreviations Used (US Version)

Before we begin, familiarize yourself with the standard US crochet abbreviations used in this pattern:

 

  * **Chain** – ch
  * **Chain stitch** – ch st
  * **Chain space** – ch sp
  * **Single crochet** – sc
  * **Slip stitch** – sl st
  * **Decrease** – dec



 

## Finished Measurement

  * Approximately **11 cm x 8 cm**



 

## Crochet Pumpkin Pattern Instructions

### Base

**Chain 26**

 

**Row 1:** 1 sc into the 2 ch from hook. 1 sc into the next 24 ch st. Turn.

 

**Row 2:** 2 ch, 1 sc into the next 24 ch st. Turn.

 

**Row 3 to Row 5:** REPEAT ROW 2.

 

**Row 6:** 2 ch, 1 sc into the next 4 ch st, 1 dec – _(SEE TUTORIAL IN TIPS & TRICKS)_, 1 sc into the next 4 ch st, 2 dec, 1 sc into the next 4 sc, 1 dec, 1 sc into the next 5 ch st. Turn.

 

**Row 7:** 2 ch, 1 sc into the next 21 ch st. Turn.

 

**Row 8 to Row 10:** REPEAT ROW 7.

 

![](https://craftedandcherished.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/Easy-Spooky.webp)

 

 

**Row 11:** 2 ch, 1 sc into the next 4 ch st, 1 dec, 1 sc into the next 8 ch st, 1 dec, 1 sc into the next 5 ch st. Turn.

 

**Row 12:** 2 ch, 1 sc into the next 18 ch st. Turn.

 

**Row 13 to Row 15:** REPEAT ROW 12.

 

**Row 16:** 2 ch, 1 sc into the next 4 ch st, 1 dec, 1 sc into the next 6 ch st, 1 dec, 1 sc into the last 5 ch st. Turn.

 

**Row 17:** 2 ch, 1 sc into the next 16 ch st. Turn.

 

**Row 18 to Row 20:** REPEAT ROW 17.

 

**Row 21:** 2 ch, 1 sc into the next 4 ch st, 1 dec, 1 sc into the next 3 ch st, 1 dec, 1 sc into the last 5 ch sp. Turn.

 

**Row 22:** 2 ch, 1 sc into the next 14 ch st. Turn.

 

**Row 23:** 2 ch, 1 sc into the next 4 ch st, 1 dec, 1 sc into the next ch st, 1 dec, 1 sc into the last 5 ch st. Turn.

 

**Row 24:** 2 ch, 1 sc into the next 12 ch st. Turn.

 

**Row 25:** 2 ch, 1 sc into the next 3 ch st, 1 dec, 1 sc into the next ch st, 1 dec, 1 sc into the next 4 ch st. Turn.

 

**Row 26:** 2 ch, 1 sc into the next 2 ch st, 1 dec, 1 sc into the next ch st, 1 dec, 1 sc into the last 3 ch st. Turn.

 

**Row 27:** 2 ch, 1 sc into next ch st, 1 dec, 1 sc into the next ch st, 1 dec, 1 sc into the last 2 ch st. Turn.

 

**Row 28:** 2 ch, 1 sc into the next ch st, 1 dec, 1 sc into the next 3 ch sp. Turn.

 

**Row 29:** 2 ch, 2 dec, 1 sc into the last ch st. Turn.

 

**Row 30:** 2 ch, 1 dec, 1 sc into the last ch st. Fasten off.

 

![](https://craftedandcherished.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/free-crochet-pumpkin-pattern.webp)

 

 

### Edging

Starting at the bottom right-hand corner of your pumpkin.

 

**Step 1:** Take your edging colour and sl st, 2 ch-2 sc into the same ch sp. _1 sc 3ch into the next ch sp_. Repeat from * to * 23 times. 3 sc into the last ch st.

 

![](https://craftedandcherished.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/free-crochet-pumpkin-pattern-Step-1.webp)

## Finishing Touches & Decorating Ideas

Repeat the process for however many pumpkins you would like to make. To give your pumpkins a crisp, professional finish, spray starch onto the pumpkin to make it more stiff. This is especially helpful if you plan to use them as coasters, as the starch prevents them from curling and protects them from minor spills.

 

### How to Use Your Crochet Pumpkins

  * **Spooky Coasters:** Keep a set by the coffee maker or on your party table to protect your surfaces in style.
  * **Halloween Bunting:** String a dozen of these stiffened pumpkins along a piece of twine or ribbon and drape them across your mantle or doorway.
