The holiday season is the perfect time to add some festive flair to your crochet projects, and what says "Merry Christmas" quite like a jolly old Saint Nick? This **Santa Granny Square pattern** takes the classic, beloved granny square and gives it a whimsical 3D twist by building a cute Santa face right in the center.

Whether you are planning to crochet a Christmas blanket, a festive holiday pillow, or a cute winter wall hanging, this Santa square is sure to be the star of the show. It uses basic crochet stitches and works up quickly, making it a perfect weekend project.

Grab your red, white, and skin-toned yarn, and let’s get crocheting!

## Materials Needed

  * **Yarn of your choice (3 colors):** The sample was made using DK/sport weight wool yarn in Red, White, and a Skin tone.
  * **Crochet Hook:** 3.50 mm hook (or whatever hook size matches your chosen yarn weight).
  * **Darning Needle:** For weaving in ends and sewing pieces together.
  * **Scissors**
  * **Black Yarn or Embroidery Floss:** For creating the French knot eyes.



## Pattern Instructions

_Note: This pattern is worked in separate pieces and then assembled. Standard US crochet terminology is used._

### Part 1: Santa’s Face

**Round 1:** Make a magic loop. Chain 2 (counts as 1 half double crochet / hdc). Make 11 hdc into the loop. Slip stitch (slst) to the top of the first chain 2 to join. (12 stitches total)

**Round 2:** Chain 3 (counts as 1 double crochet / dc). Make 1 hdc in the same stitch. Continue around with: 1 hdc, 1 hdc + 1 dc (in the next st), 1 dc + 1 hdc (in the next st), 1 dc, 1 dc + 1 hdc (in the next st), hdc increase (2 hdc in the next st), 1 dc, hdc increase, 1 hdc + 1 dc, 1 dc, 1 hdc + 1 dc. Slip stitch to the top of the first chain 3. Fasten off.

### Part 2: Full Beard

Rotate your face piece 180 degrees. Attach your white yarn to the hdc of the upper (1dc + 1hdc) group on the right side.

  * Chain 1. Single crochet (sc) increase in the next stitch, then make 1 hdc.
  * Next, work a dc2tog starting in the same stitch where you just made the hdc, and skip 1 stitch in between.
  * Work 1 hdc in the same stitch where you completed the dc2tog (the stitch that holds the 2nd leg of the dc2tog).
  * Sc increase, slst to the next stitch.



Now, continue crocheting directly onto the face edge:

  * Sc increase, 3 sc, sc increase, 3 sc, sc increase, 3 sc, sc increase.



Next, you will crochet across the beard piece you just made:

  * Skip the first chain 1. Work 1 sc + 1 hdc in the next stitch, 1 hdc + 1 dc, (dc increase) 4 times, 1 dc + 1 hdc, 1 hdc + 1 sc.
  * Slip stitch to the first sc. Fasten off.



### Part 3: Santa’s Hat

**White Trim:** With white yarn, chain 10. Return, skip 2, and working in the Back Loop Only (BLO), make 7 hdc. Chain 2, slst to the final chain. Fasten off.

**Red Hat Body:** Rotate the piece 180 degrees. Add red yarn in the right-most chain. Chain 1, 1 sc in the next chain, 5 hdc, 1 sc, chain 1, slst to the next chain.

Turn over. Chain 1 as a standing stitch. Make 3 sc, then 4 sc in the BLO. Slip stitch to join.

**Hat Brim:** Turn over. Chain 3, return, skip 1, and working in the BLO, make 1 slst in each chain around (8 slst total). Fasten off.

### Part 4: Mustache

  * **First Half:** Chain 6. Return, skip 1, and working in the BLO: slst, sc2tog, 2 hdc. Chain 1, slst to the same chain where you made the last hdc. Fasten off.
  * **Second Half:** Chain 5. Return, skip 1, and working in the BLO: slst, sc increase, 2 hdc. Chain 1, slst. Fasten off.



### Part 5: Pom-Pom & Nose

  * **Pom-Pom:** Make a magic loop. Chain 1 as a standing stitch, make 5 sc into the loop. Pull the tail tightly to close. Fasten off, leaving long tails for attaching.
  * **Nose:** Make a magic loop. Chain 1 as a standing stitch, make 4 sc into the loop. Pull the tail tightly to close. Fasten off, leaving long tails for attaching.

### Part 6: Granny Square Background

**Round 1:** Make a magic loop. Chain 3 (counts as 1 dc). Work _3 dc, chain 3_. Repeat _[4 dc, chain 3]_ 3 more times. Omit the final chain 3. To join the round, work 1 chain, then 1 hdc into the top of the first chain 3.

**Rounds 2 & 3:** Chain 3 to begin (counts as 1 dc). Make 1 dc in the same corner stitch. Work 1 dc in each stitch around the sides. In each corner, work _(2 dc, chain 3, 2 dc)_. When you reach the end of the round and return to the starting corner, make 2 dc into the starting corner. Join the round with 1 chain, then 1 hdc into the top of the beginning chain 3.

**Round 4:** Work the sides and corners exactly as you did in Rounds 2 and 3. When you return to the starting corner, work _2 dc, chain 3_ , then slst to the top of the first _true_ dc (not the chain 3). Fasten off.

### Final Assembly

  1. **Hat:** Sew the white trim and red hat body to the top of Santa’s face. Sew securely in place.
  2. **Pom-Pom:** Sew the small pom-pom to the tip of the hat so it dangles cutely beside the face.
  3. **Mustache:** Sew one mustache piece on each side of the nose, angling them outward toward the cheeks.
  4. **Nose & Eyes:** Sew the nose at the center point between the two mustaches. Finally, use black yarn or embroidery floss to make two French knots for Santa’s eyes.
