A crochet bandana might just be the fastest way to fix a bad hair day while adding a handmade touch to your look. Whether you want a triangle headscarf for the beach, a granny-square style piece for festival season, or something delicate enough for everyday wear, there's a pattern out there for every skill level and style.

This roundup pulls together 18 beginner-friendly bandana patterns — classic shapes, trendy motifs, and a few unexpected twists — so you can find your next quick project without digging through dozens of sites yourself.

 

## 1\. Granny Bandana

 

 

![](https://craftedandcherished.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/08/1.-Granny-Bandana.webp)

 

This breezy summer piece comes together in about 30 minutes flat, making it a great last-minute fix for a rough hair day. It's worked using two different stitch methods and finished off with a slightly ruffled, scalloped edge plus simple ties. [Get the pattern here.](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=GnjHEVLptl8)

## 2\. Crochet Head Kerchief

 

![](https://craftedandcherished.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/08/2.-Crochet-Head-Kerchief.webp)

 

Built with basic double crochet stitches, this triangle-shaped bandana is designed with non-curling borders and lightweight yarn — perfect for keeping the sun off your head without feeling heavy. Swap in your favorite yarn color for an easy personal touch. [Get the pattern here.](https://blog.treasurie.com/crochet-bandana/)

## 3\. Tunisian Bandana

 

![](https://craftedandcherished.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/08/3.-Tunisian-Bandana.webp)

 

A great pick if you're curious about Tunisian crochet but not ready for anything complex — this design relies mainly on simple and purl stitches, with no strict gauge required. Work up a small swatch first if you want to double-check your tension. [Get the pattern here.](https://yarnandy.com/beginner-crochet-bandana-free-pattern/)

## 4\. Granny Square Bandana

 

![](https://craftedandcherished.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/08/4.-Granny-Square-Bandana.webp)

 

A tidy, wearable take on the classic granny square, made with just DK yarn, a 4.0mm hook, a yarn needle, and scissors. It's a nice low-fuss project if you already have granny square basics down and want to turn them into something wearable. [Get the pattern here.](https://www.lullabylodge.com/2025/10/how-to-crochet-granny-square-bandana.html)

## 5\. Pink Bandana

 

![](https://craftedandcherished.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/08/5.-Pink-Bandana.webp)

 

Light and airy, this triangle headscarf is worked in cotton yarn, making it especially well-suited to spring and summer wear. It's a versatile pick for the beach, an outdoor festival, or just running errands in warm weather. [Get the pattern here.](https://www.ravelry.com/patterns/library/easy-bandana-4)

## 6\. Shell Stitch Bandana

 

![](https://craftedandcherished.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/08/6.-Shell-Stitch-Bandana.webp)

 

Leaning into a cottagecore look, this bandana uses the shell stitch as its main texture for a soft, boho feel. It's a great pattern for beginners who want to practice foundational shell-stitch skills while making something they'll actually wear. [Get the pattern here.](https://www.selinaveronique.com/crochet-cottagecore-inspired-bandana-free-pattern)

## 7\. Daisy Bandana

 

![](https://craftedandcherished.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/08/7.-Daisy-Bandana.webp)

 

If cheerful floral motifs are your thing, this daisy-inspired bandana is an easy, approachable project for beginners who are ready for a slightly more adventurous pattern. The daisy detail gives it a fresh, spring-ready feel. [Get the pattern here.](https://crochetbymery.com/2024/04/easy-beginner-crochet-bandana.html)

## 8\. Sweetheart Bandana

 

![](https://craftedandcherished.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/08/8.-Sweetheart-Bandana.webp)

 

This heart-motif design is a good option once you're comfortable with basic bandana shaping and ready to build advanced-beginner skills. It uses bobbins to keep the color work clean, along with a few other simple supplies. [Get the pattern here.](https://madefullycrochet.com/sweetheart-crochet-bandana-free-pattern/#mv-creation-1-jtr)

## 9\. Lilly Bandana

 

![](https://craftedandcherished.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/08/9.-Lilly-Bandana.webp)

 

Another cottagecore-inspired piece, this lily-themed bandana is refreshingly simple to work up while still looking polished once finished. It sits nicely on the head and makes a sweet everyday accessory. [Get the pattern here.](https://www.selinaveronique.com/crochet-lilly-cottagecore-bandana-free-pattern)

## 10\. Vintage Bandana

 

![](https://craftedandcherished.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/08/10.-Vintage-Bandana.webp)

 

With a delicate, variegated look, this bandana brings in a bit of old-world charm. It's a relaxing project that works well as background stitching while you watch TV or unwind after a long day. [Get the pattern here.](https://aboxoftwine.com/free-crochet-bandana-pattern/)

## 11\. DIY Leaf Bandana

 

![](https://craftedandcherished.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/08/11.-DIY-Leaf-Bandana.webp)

 

Simple, pretty, and easy to wear on repeat — this leaf-motif design uses just a handful of basic stitches: chain, slip stitch, single crochet, and treble crochet. It's the kind of low-effort, high-reward project you'll want to make more than once. [Get the pattern here.](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=4A7QRc71Je0)

## 12\. DIY Granny Square Pattern

 

![](https://craftedandcherished.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/08/12.-DIY-Granny-Square-Pattern.webp)

 

Made from a cluster of small, colorful granny squares, this fun bandana taps into current trend territory while staying totally beginner-friendly. It's an easy choice for festivals, beach days, or casual weekend outings. [Get the pattern here.](https://www.anniedesigncrochet.com/granny-square-bandana-free-crochet-pattern/)

## 13\. Sunflower Bandana Pattern

 

![](https://craftedandcherished.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/08/13.-Sunflower-Bandana-Pattern.webp)

 

Sunflowers are the ultimate warm-weather motif, and this bandana makes the most of that cheerful energy. The stitching is straightforward, the finished look is eye-catching, and it's the kind of piece that tends to draw compliments every time you wear it. [Get the pattern here.](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=8o-LnRSpLIc)

## 14\. Fringe Bandana

 

![](https://craftedandcherished.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/08/14.-Fringe-Bandana.webp)

 

Built from a cluster of small, colorful granny-square flowers, this design gets an extra boost of personality from the fringe detailing along the back edge. It's a fun way to combine floral motifs with a little movement and texture. [Get the pattern here.](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=1Hh0zPotOcE)

## 15\. Simple Hair Accessory

 

![](https://craftedandcherished.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/08/15.-Simple-Hair-Accessory.webp)

 

This one's ideal if you've got leftover yarn scraps to use up. The pattern comes with a detailed, easy-to-follow tutorial that's approachable even if you've genuinely never picked up a crochet hook before — and there's a video to walk you through it step by step. [Get the pattern here.](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=nNiGATgo_hA)

## 16\. Picot Crochet Bandana

 

![](https://craftedandcherished.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/08/16.-Picot-Crochet-Bandana.webp)

 

Featuring dainty picot detailing, this bandana pattern comes in three different sizes, so you can pick your fit or even make a few in different sizes as matching accessories for family or friends. [Get the pattern here.](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=QWSu8NZlrLE)

## 17\. Triangle Bandana

 

![](https://craftedandcherished.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/08/17.-Triangle-Bandana.webp)

 

Fast and genuinely beginner-friendly, this triangle granny-stitch bandana can be finished in under three hours — even if you've never crocheted before. ## 18\. Handcrafted Head Scarf

 

![](https://craftedandcherished.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/08/16.-Picot-Crochet-Bandana-1.webp)

 

Worked in soft milk cotton yarn with a 3.5mm hook, this minimalist bandana keeps things simple while still feeling special, thanks to two small flower accents attached at the ends of the ties. A little detail that goes a long way. [Get the pattern here.](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=O38dtutYFwk)

## Final Thoughts

From quick 30-minute makes to slightly more detailed floral and granny-square designs, this list has a beginner-friendly bandana pattern for pretty much any mood, season, or occasion. Pick one that matches your current skill level, grab your favorite yarn, and you'll have a new handmade accessory ready in no time.
