## Materials Needed

The pattern uses worsted-weight cotton yarn, which is ideal for summer garments because it is breathable, durable, and lightweight. Cotton also helps the top maintain its shape after washing.

### Supplies

  * Worsted cotton yarn
  * 5 mm crochet hook
  * Darning needle
  * Scissors
  * Stitch markers (optional but helpful)



### Yarn Amounts by Size

The pattern includes sizes XS through XL and requires approximately:

  * XS: 350 m
  * S: 375 m
  * M: 425 m
  * L: 475 m
  * XL: 550 m



Choosing soft cotton yarn with good drape will give the top a polished, wearable finish.

## Crochet Stitches Used

This project combines beginner-friendly stitches with decorative shaping techniques. The stitches include:

  * Chain stitch (ch)
  * Slip stitch (slst)
  * Single crochet (sc)
  * Half double crochet (hdc)
  * Double crochet (dc)
  * Treble crochet (tr)
  * Double crochet two together (dc2tog)
  * Treble crochet two together (tr2tog)



Because the top uses repetitive stitch patterns, it becomes relaxing and rhythmic once the setup rounds are complete.

## Understanding the Construction

The crochet top begins with a circular yoke worked in closed rounds. Decorative openwork rounds create the airy neckline detail before short rows are added to shape the front and back sections.

After the yoke is complete:

  1. Back short rows add extra coverage and comfort
  2. Front short rows shape the neckline
  3. Underarm chains create armholes
  4. The bodice is worked in rounds to the desired length



This construction method eliminates bulky seams and creates a clean professional finish.

## Tips Before You Start

### Check Your Gauge

The pattern gauge is:

14 dc × 8 rows = 4 × 4 inches

Checking gauge before beginning helps ensure the finished top fits properly. If your stitches are too tight, move up a hook size. If too loose, use a smaller hook.

### Use Stitch Markers

Because the pattern works continuously in rounds, stitch markers can help track the beginning of rounds and underarm sections.

### Try It On Frequently

One of the biggest advantages of top-down crochet garments is flexibility. You can easily adjust:

  * Body length
  * Waist shaping
  * Armhole depth
  * Overall fit



Trying the garment on during construction helps create a customized fit.

## Yoke and Openwork Details

The yoke section creates the signature look of this crochet top. Openwork rounds alternate with textured stitches to form an airy neckline that feels elegant without being overly complicated.

This section may look intricate at first glance, but the repeating stitch pattern becomes intuitive after a few rounds.

The openwork detail also helps reduce weight around the neckline, making the top especially comfortable during warm weather.

## Front and Back Short Rows Explained

Short rows can intimidate beginners, but they are simply partial rows used to shape the garment.

In this pattern:

  * Back short rows create a comfortable raised back neckline
  * Front short rows shape the front opening for a flattering fit



These shaping techniques improve comfort and prevent the neckline from pulling backward while wearing the top.

If you are new to short rows, count carefully and use stitch markers to stay organized.

## Creating the Bodice

Once the armholes are formed, the bodice becomes highly repetitive and relaxing to crochet. Decorative underarm mesh panels continue throughout the body, creating airflow and visual texture.

The pattern repeats specific bodice rounds until the desired length is reached, making it easy to customize for:

  * Cropped tops
  * Standard tank tops
  * Tunic-length tops



For a more modern oversized look, you can even add extra length and slight positive ease.

## Optional Waist Shaping

The optional shaping instructions allow crocheters to create a more fitted silhouette by gradually decreasing and increasing stitches around the waist and hips.

This is especially useful if you prefer:

  * A more tailored fit
  * A flattering waistline
  * A closer body-hugging shape



However, the top also looks beautiful worn relaxed and slightly loose.

## Blocking and Finishing Tips

Blocking is especially important for cotton garments because it improves stitch definition and drape.

### To Block Your Top

  1. Gently wash the garment
  2. Remove excess water with a towel
  3. Lay flat on blocking mats
  4. Shape carefully
  5. Allow to dry completely



Blocking helps open the lace sections and smooth uneven stitches for a polished final look.
