Crochet flowers are endlessly versatile — pin them to a hat, sew them onto a bag, join them into a granny square, or wire them up into a full bouquet that never wilts. This roundup gathers 20 free **crochet flower patterns** from some of our favorite designers, ranging from quick scrap-yarn blooms to detailed, wire-stemmed roses built for a vase. Whatever your skill level, there's a flower here worth adding to your queue.

 

## 1\. Crochet Loopy Flower

![Crochet Loopy Flower](https://www.crochet365knittoo.com/wp-content/uploads/2017/07/Loopy-Flower.jpg)

This loopy flower pattern is an easy, fun way to use up small amounts of scrap yarn, with a textured, ruffled look that comes together in just a few rounds. It's a favorite for embellishing hats, bags, and pillows. [See the full Loopy Flower pattern here](https://www.crochet365knittoo.com/crochet-loopy-flower-pattern/) to start stitching your own.

## 2\. Crochet African Flower

![Crochet African Flower](https://www.crochet365knittoo.com/wp-content/uploads/2019/10/African-Flower-Square-1.jpg)

This African Flower tutorial covers both the traditional hexagon motif and a petal variation, making it a great two-in-one pattern for anyone building toward a full afghan. [Follow the full African Flower tutorial](https://www.crochet365knittoo.com/crochet-african-flower/) to learn both versions of this classic motif.

## 3\. Crochet Wallflower

![crochet flower](https://www.crochet365knittoo.com/wp-content/uploads/2020/07/crochet-wallflower-featured.jpg)

The Flower Power Wallflower is a fast, oversized flower motif designed to be framed or hung as wall art rather than worn, proving crochet flowers don't have to stay small. [Check out the full Wallflower pattern](https://www.crochet365knittoo.com/crochet-wallflower/) to make a statement piece for your walls.

## 4\. Crochet Black-Eyed Susan Flower

![How to crochet black eyed susan flower](https://www.crochet365knittoo.com/wp-content/uploads/2018/04/blackeyedsusanfeatured.webp)

This cheerful Black-Eyed Susan comes together with basic stitches, a popcorn-and-picot center, and a simple button, taking about 15 minutes from start to finish. [Get the full Black-Eyed Susan pattern](https://www.crochet365knittoo.com/crochet-black-eyed-susan-flower/) to make this quick, bright bloom.

## 5\. Changeable Crochet Flower Headband

![changeable crochet flower headband](https://www.crochet365knittoo.com/wp-content/uploads/2019/06/Easy-headband.jpg)

This clever headband pattern works like a recipe, so you can size it to fit any head and swap flowers in and out while the button closure stays put. It's a fun way to get more mileage out of your other crochet flower patterns. [Follow the full Changeable Flower Headband pattern](https://www.crochet365knittoo.com/changeable-crochet-flower-headband/) to make one headband with endless flower options.

## 6\. 3D Sunflower Granny Square

![3D Sunflower Granny Square Pattern](https://craftedandcherished.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/08/sunflower-granny-square-pattern-825x1024.webp)

This dimensional sunflower granny square uses front-and-back-loop stitching to build a raised, textured seed center surrounded by pointed, picot-tipped petals, all set on a classic granny square border. It's a rewarding project for intermediate crocheters who love botanical, textured motifs. [See the full 3D Sunflower Granny Square pattern here](https://craftedandcherished.com/sunflower-granny-square-pattern/) for the complete instructions.

## 7\. Skull and Flower Granny Square

![skull and flower granny square](https://craftedandcherished.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/08/skull-and-flower-granny-square-822x1024.webp)

This one's for crocheters who like their florals with an edge: a square frame, rounded 3D petals, and a skull motif combine for a design that works beautifully in gothic, pastel, or Halloween color palettes. [Check out the full Skull and Flower Granny Square pattern here](https://craftedandcherished.com/skull-and-flower-granny-square/) to try this striking mashup.

From quick scrap-yarn blooms to detailed, wire-stemmed roses, these 20 crochet flower patterns offer something for every skill level and every project — hats, bags, granny squares, or a full standalone bouquet. Pick a few that catch your eye, and you'll have a beautiful handmade garden that never needs watering.
