## Which Clementine Crochet Pattern Should You Try First?

The best project depends on what you want to make.

For a **simple clementine crochet pattern free** , the small amigurumi designs from Doriyumi and Hello Yellow Yarn are excellent places to begin.

If you prefer realistic crochet food, try the Stitched Safari orange and clementine or the PlanetJune Citrus Collection.

For something practical, make the Clementine Scrubby.

And if you're planning Lunar New Year decorations or want a larger statement piece, the crochet clementine tree offers a completely different way to use these little crocheted fruits.

Beginners shouldn't be intimidated by the round shape. Most amigurumi citrus patterns rely on the same basic construction: start with a magic ring, increase until the fruit reaches the desired width, crochet several even rounds, decrease, add stuffing, and close the opening.

Once you're comfortable making one, it's easy to create an entire basket of crochet clementines.
