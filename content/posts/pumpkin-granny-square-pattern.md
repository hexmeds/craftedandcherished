Welcome the cozy vibes of autumn into your crochet projects with this charming pumpkin granny square! Featuring a plump, rust-orange pumpkin complete with a curved black stem, this motif sits beautifully against a cream background and a neutral beige border. It is the ultimate building block for your fall-themed makes.

This pattern is a fantastic project for intermediate crocheters who want to practice shaping and colorwork, though adventurous beginners will definitely enjoy the process too. Whether you are planning a seasonal throw blanket, a festive table runner, or a simple autumn garland, this square brings instant warmth to any space.

_This pattern uses USA crochet terminology._

## Materials Needed

  * Yarn of your choice (4 colors)
  * Crochet hook
  * Darning needle
  * Scissors



## Abbreviations Used

  * **ch** = chain
  * **sc** = single crochet
  * **hdc** = half double crochet
  * **dc** = double crochet
  * **slst** = slip stitch
  * **inc** = increase (e.g., sc inc, hdc inc, dc inc)
  * **tog** = together (e.g., hdc2tog)
  * **blo** = back loop only
  * **sp** = space
  * **st(s)** = stitch(es)



## Pattern Instructions

### The Pumpkin Body

 

![Pumpkin Granny Square Pattern](https://craftedandcherished.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/08/Pumpkin-Granny-Square-Pattern-1.webp)

 

**Round 1- Pumpkin:** Magic loop, ch3 as standing st, 6hdc, 2dc, 1hdc, 6sc, 1hdc, 1dc, tighten tail, slst to top of ch3.

**Round 2:** ch2(count as hdc), another hdc in same st to form a hdc inc, 6dc, hdc inc.

**Round 3:** Turn work, ch2(count as hdc), 9hdc.

**Round 4:** Turn work, ch2(count as hdc)+hdc to form a hdc2tog, 6hdc, hdc2tog.

**Round 5:** Turn work, ch2(count as hdc), 6hdc, ch2, slst to top of ch2.

**Round 6:** Turn work, ch2(count as hdc), (hdc+ch2+hdc+dc) in same st, 6dc, (dc+hdc+ch2+2hdc) in next st.Working around, make 2dc, dc inc, 2dc, (hdc+sc)in next st, ch2, sc inc, sc, 4 slip stitches, sc, sc inc, ch2, (sc+hdc) in next st, 2dc, dc inc, 2dc, slst to top of ch2, _fasten off_.

### The Pumpkin Stem

**Round 7- Pumpkin Stem:** Turn work and join black yarn in blo sc st of round 1, ch7, (sc inc, 3sc, hdc inc, dc) in 2nd to last ch, sk 3, slst in blo st(left side of join), _fasten off_ , pull the stem through the circle round 1 formed.

### The Background & Border

**Round 8:** Add white yarn in blo of sc inc st of round 6, ch2(count as hdc), 9hdc, _(hdc+ch2+hdc) in next ch-sp, hdc, 8sc, (hdc+ch2+hdc) in next ch-sp_ , 10hdc, rep from _to_ , slst to top of ch2.

**Round 9:** ch2(count as hdc), 11hdc, _(2hdc+ch2+2hdc) in next ch-sp, [12hdc, rep_] rep 3 times, slst to top of ch2.

**Round 10:** ch2(count as hdc), 13hdc, _(2hdc+ch2+2hdc) in next ch-sp, [14hdc, rep_] rep 3 times, slst to top of ch2, _fasten off_.

**Round 11:** Add new yarn in corner sp, ch2(count as hdc), (hdc+ch2+2hdc) in same ch-sp, 20hdc, [(2hdc+ch2+2hdc) in next ch-sp, 20hdc] rep 3 times, slst to top of ch2, _fasten off and weave in ends_.

## Tips

  * **Yarn Selection:** While you can use any yarn you have on hand, using a cotton or cotton-blend worsted weight yarn will help your pumpkin hold its structured, plump shape beautifully without stretching out over time.
  * **Color Customization:** The classic rust-orange, black, cream, and beige palette is stunning for fall, but you could easily swap the orange for white to make a ghost, or green to make an apple!
