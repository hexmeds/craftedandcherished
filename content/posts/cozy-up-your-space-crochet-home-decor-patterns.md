## Easy Crochet Projects for Beginners

You do not need to be an expert--promise. Try one of these (I did, in a snowstorm, while baking banana bread):

  * **Coasters** --yep, you can make 'em during one episode of your favorite show.
  * Simple square **blankets** --no wild math, just back-and-forth rows.
  * Little wall **hangings** --screams "boho," but is really just a rectangle.



Even if your stitches aren't perfect (mine sure aren't), it just adds to the personality.

## Tips and Tools for Successful Crochet Projects

Before you get all tangled up (literally), here are some quick pointers:

  * Use thick yarn if you want to see results fast--trust me, patience isn't my virtue either.
  * Big hooks = big stitches = big progress.
  * Keep a good pair of **scissors** nearby (those little yarn bits crawl everywhere!).
  * Don't stress about perfection--it's supposed to be fun, not a five-star restaurant review.



Go slow, laugh off the mistakes, and keep a snack nearby. You got this!
