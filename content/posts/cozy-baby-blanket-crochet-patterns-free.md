## Common Questions

**How long does it REALLY take to crochet a baby blanket?**  
Depends, but with chunky yarn--a weekend binge-watch and you're golden!

**Can I use leftover yarn from other projects?**  
Of course! Just keep the texture and weight the same-ish or it'll look, well, kinda strange.

**Do I have to block my finished blanket?**  
Honestly, for gifts--yes. It levels everything out and feels more "polished." Still, sometimes I skip if I'm in a rush.

**What size hook do I use for chunky yarn?**  
Usually a size K (6.5mm) or bigger. If your blanket looks stiff, try going up a hook size.

**Is shell stitch hard for beginners?**  
Not at all. Shell stitch is repetitive, and after a few rows your hands basically go on autopilot.
