There is nothing quite like curling up under a handmade blanket while watching your favorite classic horror movies. This Halloween-Themed Crochet Blanket takes that cozy experience to a whole new level by combining the simple comfort of a solid gray throw with playful, spooky appliqués. Featuring grinning jack-o'-lanterns, friendly ghosts, soaring bats, and tiny witch hats, this blanket is a festive statement piece for the autumn season.

This project is highly approachable for advanced beginners who know how to work basic stitches in rows and rounds. The blanket base is a relaxing, rhythmic project, while the small motifs offer a fun chance to play with color and simple embroidery. If you enjoy making smaller spooky decor items like [Halloween crochet kitchen towels](https://craftedandcherished.com/halloween-crochet-kitchen-towels/) or an [amigurumi Ghostface](https://craftedandcherished.com/amigurumi-ghostface-pattern/), you will absolutely love adding this blanket to your festive crafting queue.

## Materials Needed

  * **Yarn:** Orange, black, white, gray, green, yellow (Worsted weight recommended)
  * **Crochet hook:** 5.0 mm
  * **Tapestry needle:** For sewing appliqués and weaving in ends
  * **Scissors**



## Abbreviations Used

  * **ch** - chain
  * **sc** - single crochet
  * **dc** - double crochet
  * **hdc** - half double crochet
  * **sl st** - slip stitch
  * **inc** - increase
  * **dec** - decrease
  * **MR** - magic ring



_This pattern uses USA crochet terminology_

## Pattern Instructions

### Blanket Base

**Foundation:** Using gray yarn, start with a chain that measures the width of your bed (e.g., ch 200).

**Row 1:** Dc in each ch across. Ch 2, turn.

**Rows 2-80:** Continue dc in rows until the desired length is reached.

**Border:** With black yarn, sc evenly around the edges of the blanket for a clean finish.

### Pumpkin Appliqué

_(Using Orange Yarn)_

**Round 1:** Start with MR, 6 sc in the ring (6).

**Round 2:** Inc in each st (12).

**Round 3:** 1 sc, inc around (18).

**Round 4:** 2 sc, inc around (24).

**Round 5-9:** Sc in each st (24), fasten off.

**Pumpkin Face (Black Yarn):**

Use black yarn to embroider the jack-o’-lantern face with triangle eyes and a jagged smile.

### Ghost Motif

_(Using White Yarn)_

**Round 1:** MR, 6 sc in the ring (6).

**Round 2:** Inc in each st (12).

**Round 3:** 1 sc, inc around (18).

**Round 4:** 2 sc, inc around (24).

**Round 5-7:** Sc in each st to form the ghost’s body.

Create a wavy edge by alternating sc and hdc along the bottom edge of the ghost. Fasten off.

**Ghost Eyes and Mouth (Black Yarn):**

Embroider simple ovals for eyes and a small mouth on the ghost’s face.

### Bat Motif

_(Using Black Yarn)_

**Bat Body:** Start with MR, 6 sc in the ring (6).

**Round 2:** Inc in each st (12).

**Round 3-5:** Sc in each st to form the bat’s body. Fasten off.

**Bat Wings:** Ch 10, sc back along the chain to form wings. Make two wings for each bat and sew to the body.

### Witch Hat

_(Using Black Yarn)_

**Round 1:** MR, 6 sc (6).

**Round 2:** 1 sc, inc (9).

**Round 3:** 2 sc, inc (12).

**Round 4-7:** Continue sc in each round until the cone shape forms.

Attach a small pumpkin or buckle detail on the witch hat using orange or yellow yarn.

## Finishing

Attach all appliqué pieces—jack-o’-lantern, ghosts, bats, witch hats—across the blanket as per your desired layout.

Add any additional embroidery or small appliqué details, such as stars, moons, or other Halloween-themed symbols.

_Note: Here is a pattern suggestion for you to create your own pattern. The end result may vary._

## Tips

  * **Layout Before You Sew:** Before you thread your tapestry needle, arrange all of your pumpkins, ghosts, bats, and hats across the blanket. Take a picture with your phone so you have a visual map to follow as you sew them down. This prevents crooked motifs and ensures your spacing is perfectly even.
  * **Sturdy Appliqué Attachments:** Because this blanket will likely be used and washed, make sure to tack down your appliqués securely. Weave your yarn through the back loops of the blanket base multiple times, paying special attention to the corners of the witch hats and the edges of the bat wings.
  * **Mix and Match Decor:** If you find yourself with leftover black yarn after making your bats and witch hats, try pairing this blanket with other spooky home accessories like a [crochet spiderweb bunting](https://craftedandcherished.com/crochet-spiderweb-bunting-pattern/) draped over your mantel. ## FAQ

**Can I make this blanket smaller or larger?** Absolutely! To change the size, simply adjust your starting foundation chain. Just remember that your starting chain should be an even number to keep your double crochet rows aligned. You can also stop rowing whenever you reach your desired length.

**What type of yarn is best for this project?** A durable, washable acrylic or a soft cotton-acrylic blend in a worsted weight is ideal. Since this is a blanket that may see a lot of use during the fall movie season, you want a yarn that is easy to care for and won't pill excessively.

**Do I have to use gray for the blanket base?** Not at all! While gray provides a great neutral backdrop that makes the orange and white motifs pop, you could easily use a deep purple, dark green, or even a stark black base for a darker, more dramatic look.
