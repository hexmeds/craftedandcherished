# Cabled Peplum Tee Free Crochet Pattern

Crocheting is an incredible way to express creativity while crafting wearable art. The Cabled Peplum Tee pattern from Two Brothers Blankets beautifully embodies this art form by blending texture and style. This free crochet pattern is perfect for both beginners and seasoned crocheters looking for a charming addition to their wardrobe. Not only does this tee offer a lovely silhouette, but it’s also a cozy piece that can be worn in any season.

## Overview of the Cabled Peplum Tee

The Cabled Peplum Tee is designed with a delicate cabled texture that adds depth and interest to this simple yet captivating piece. Its peplum style allows it to flatter various body types, providing an elegant fit for casual or dressier occasions. Whether you’re heading out for brunch or lounging at home, this tee is a stylish option that will quickly become a favorite in your collection.

### What You Will Need

Before diving into your crochet project, gather all necessary materials to ensure a smooth crafting experience. Here’s a list of what you will need:

  * **Yarn:** Choose a medium weight (#4) yarn. Cotton or a cotton blend works great for breathability.
  * **Crochet Hook:** Size I (5.5 mm) or size needed to obtain gauge.
  * **Scissors:** For cutting yarn.
  * **Tapestry Needle:** To weave in ends.
  * **Stitch Markers:** Helpful for marking your place in the pattern.
  * **Measuring Tape:** To ensure perfect fit and dimensions.



### Skill Level

This pattern is suitable for intermediate crocheters. However, with clear instructions and a bit of patience, beginners may find it a fun challenge. Familiarity with basic stitches such as single crochet, double crochet, and the cable stitch will help in completing this project smoothly.

## Gauge

Before starting, checking the gauge is crucial for the perfect fit. The pattern typically requires a gauge of 10 sts x 12 rows = 4” in a cabled pattern. Adjust your hook size if necessary to get the correct gauge.

## Stitch Abbreviations

  * **ch:** chain
  * **sc:** single crochet
  * **dc:** double crochet
  * **st:** stitch
  * **RS:** right side
  * **WS:** wrong side
  * **sk:** skip
  * **cbl:** cable stitch



## Cabled Peplum Tee Pattern

### Starting with the Body

  1. **Foundation Chain:** Begin by creating a foundation chain of 120 (or size needed for your fit), making sure that it's a multiple of 10 plus 3.
  2. **Row 1:** Work in the first row using single crochet across the chain. Turn.
  3. **Row 2:** For this row, create a cabled stitch. (Here’s a basic cable stitch instruction: Sk 4, dc in the next 2 stitches, then back to the skipped stitches and dc in them as well. Finally, pull the stitches together to complete the cable.)
  4. **Repeat Rows:** Continue to work in rows, alternating between solid stitches and cable stitches, until the piece measures approximately 16 inches from the foundation chain.



### Creating the Peplum

  1. **Peplum Start:** Once you reach the desired body length, start the peplum section. Begin by chaining 3 and double crocheting in every stitch across the last row.
  2. **Increase Peplum:** For the second row of peplum, incorporate increases. You’ll want to double crochet in every fifth stitch for the peplum to flare out beautifully.
  3. **Row Continuation:** Continue the pattern until the peplum is around 8 inches long or to your desired length.

### Shaping the Armholes

Once the peplum is complete, it’s time to shape the armholes:

  1. **Divide for Armholes:** Find the center of your piece and mark it. Count outwards on both sides. You will be skipping a certain amount of stitches for the armholes.
  2. **Shaping:** Crochet straight for a few rows until you have your desired length to the shoulder.
  3. **Repeat for Both Sides:** Ensure that both shoulders match in length and shape by repeating the process on the opposite side.



### Finalizing the Tee

Now that you have completed the body and the peplum, it’s time for the finishing touches:

  1. **Neckline:** Using single crochet, create a simple edging for the neckline.
  2. **Sleeves (Optional):** If you want to add additional fabric to the sleeves, simply crochet an extra panel on each side, using the existing pattern.
  3. **Weaving Ends:** Use a tapestry needle to weave in all loose ends, ensuring a clean finish.



### Blocking Your Peplum Tee

Once your Cabled Peplum Tee is complete, blocking is a wonderful way to enhance the look of your piece. Gently wet your garment and lay it flat on a surface. Pin it into place to keep the shape. Blocking not only helps even out stitches but also highlighted the beautiful cable details.
