If you are hunting for your next quick, stylish, and stash-busting project, Ravelry is an absolute goldmine for crochet patterns. Crochet bandanas and hair scarves are the ultimate accessory—they tame flyaways on windy days, add a pop of color to a basic outfit, and make fantastic, wearable gifts.

 

We’ve scoured Ravelry to bring you 9 stunning, **free crochet bandana patterns** that belong on your hook right now. Whether you prefer breezy mesh stitches or cozy, textured kerchiefs, there is a pattern here for you. Let’s check them out!

 

 

## 1\. Simple Bandana

![](https://craftedandcherished.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/1-Simple-Bandana.webp)

Sometimes, you just need a classic, no-fuss design. The Simple Bandana is exactly that—a perfectly proportioned, beginner-friendly kerchief that works up in just an evening. Its minimalist design makes it the perfect canvas to experiment with self-striping or variegated yarns, letting the colors do all the talking!

 

[**👉 Get the Pattern**](https://www.ravelry.com/patterns/library/simple-bandana-2)

![](https://craftedandcherished.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/2-Simple-bandana.webp)

## 3\. Mesh Bandana

![](https://craftedandcherished.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/3-MESH-Bandana.webp)

When the temperatures rise, you don't want a heavy crochet piece on your head. The Mesh Bandana is the ultimate summer hair accessory! Featuring an open, breathable mesh stitch pattern, this kerchief allows the air to flow through while keeping your hair stylishly secured. It's a beach-bag essential.

 

[**👉 Get the Pattern**](https://www.ravelry.com/patterns/library/mesh-bandana-3)

 

## 4\. Country Road Bandana

![](https://craftedandcherished.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/4-Country-Road-Bandana.webp)

Channel your inner rustic romantic with the Country Road Bandana. This pattern brings a touch of farmhouse charm to your wardrobe. With a highly textured stitch that looks like it belongs in a modern country boutique, this bandana is cozy, stylish, and perfect for pairing with your favorite denim jacket.

 

[**👉 Get the Pattern**](https://www.ravelry.com/patterns/library/country-road-bandana)

 

## 5\. Garden Vines Bandana

![](https://craftedandcherished.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/5-Garden-Vines-Bandana.webp)

It’s an elegant, lacy design that adds an instant touch of feminine grace to any outfit, whether you're heading to brunch or a garden party.

 

[**👉 Get the Pattern**](https://www.ravelry.com/patterns/library/garden-vines-bandana)

 

## 6\. The Weekend Bandana

![](https://craftedandcherished.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/6-The-Weekend-Bandana.webp)
