# Skill Level

**Beginner**

The pattern mainly uses basic crochet stitches worked in the round. Since the top is made-to-measure, there’s no complicated sizing math involved, making it an approachable project even for newer crocheters.

# Materials Needed

Before starting your crochet mesh top, gather the following supplies.

## Yarn

  * DK weight cotton yarn
  * Approximately 4 balls for a long-sleeved medium-size top
  * Add extra yarn for larger sizes



Cotton yarn works especially well because it helps the mesh stitches hold their shape while remaining breathable and comfortable.

## Recommended Hook

  * 4.5 mm crochet hook



## Additional Supplies

  * 4 stitch markers
  * Darning needle
  * Scissors
  * Measuring tape



# Crochet Abbreviations

This pattern uses UK crochet terminology.

### Common Abbreviations

  * ch – chain
  * ch-sp – chain space
  * dc – double crochet
  * dtr – double treble crochet
  * ss – slip stitch
  * rnd – round
  * rep – repeat
  * sm – stitch marker
  * inc – increase



If you are more familiar with US crochet terms, be sure to convert stitches before starting the project.

# How the Crochet Top Is Constructed

This top is worked from the neckline downward in continuous rounds. The raglan increases create the yoke and shape the shoulders naturally.

The body and sleeves are then separated, allowing you to customize the fit and sleeve length easily.

One of the biggest advantages of top-down crochet garments is that you can try them on as you work. This makes it easier to create a flattering fit without complicated calculations.

# Starting the Neckline

Begin by chaining enough stitches to comfortably fit over your head. The original pattern uses 90 chains as a reference.

Be careful not to twist the chain before joining into a round.

Once joined, fold the neckline in half and place stitch markers to create the raglan increase points. These markers help define the front, back, and sleeves of the top.

### Beginner Tip

Place removable stitch markers in bright colors so they are easier to spot while crocheting.

# Creating the Mesh Stitch Pattern

The signature mesh design is formed using repeated chain spaces and double treble crochet stitches.

As you continue working the rounds:

  * Chain spaces create the open mesh look
  * Raglan increases shape the garment
  * The yoke gradually expands toward the underarms



Continue repeating the mesh rounds until the yoke comfortably reaches your armpits.

Trying the top on regularly is highly recommended to ensure the perfect fit.

# Separating the Sleeves

Once the yoke is complete, the sleeves are separated from the body by chaining underneath the arms to create armholes.

This step transforms the project from a yoke into a wearable garment shape.

At this stage, you can already see the final silhouette of your crochet top.

# Working the Body

After the sleeves are separated, continue working the mesh stitch pattern in rounds for the body section.

You can customize the length easily:

  * Crop top length
  * Waist length
  * Tunic style
  * Oversized beach cover-up



The repetitive stitch pattern makes this part relaxing and meditative.

# How to Customize the Fit

One of the best parts of this crochet mesh top pattern is how customizable it is.

## For a More Fitted Look

Decrease stitches near the underarm area to create shaping around the waist.

This creates a more tailored silhouette.

## For an Oversized Style

Add increases at the sides to make the top looser and more relaxed.

Oversized crochet mesh tops are especially trendy for layering over tank tops and swimsuits.

# Optional Finishing Touches

To give the garment a cleaner, more polished finish, you can add extra edging around:

  * Neckline
  * Sleeves
  * Hemline



The original pattern suggests adding a round of double crochet stitches for more structure.

This simple finishing detail can dramatically improve the overall look of the garment.

# Crochet Sleeves

The sleeves are worked directly onto the armholes after the body is completed.

You can make:

  * Three-quarter sleeves
  * Long bell sleeves



Because the sleeves use the same mesh repeat, they work up quickly and maintain a cohesive design.

# Best Yarn Choices for Mesh Crochet Tops

While cotton DK yarn is an excellent choice, several other yarn fibers also work well for mesh garments.

## Recommended Fibers

### Cotton

  * Breathable
  * Great stitch definition
  * Ideal for warm weather



### Bamboo Blends

  * Soft drape
  * Lightweight feel
  * Slight sheen



### Cotton Acrylic Blends

  * More affordable

  * Good durability



Avoid overly fuzzy yarns because they can hide the beautiful open mesh texture.

# Tips for Success

## Measure Frequently

Because this is a made-to-measure crochet pattern, regular fitting checks are important.

## Count Carefully

Mesh stitch patterns are repetitive, which makes it easy to accidentally skip stitches.

## Use Stitch Markers

Markers help maintain accurate raglan shaping.

## Block the Finished Garment

Light blocking helps open the mesh stitches beautifully and improves drape.
