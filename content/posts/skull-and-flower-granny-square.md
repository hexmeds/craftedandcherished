Crochet meets edgy aesthetics in this unique Skull and Flower Granny Square. This pattern brilliantly combines geometric shapes—a square frame, rounded 3D petals, and a central skull motif—for a truly distinctive design that stands out from traditional squares.

It's a fantastic project for intermediate crocheters looking to expand their stitch repertoire. The dimensional effect created by the clever stitch combinations in the petals makes this square incredibly satisfying to make. Whether you're creating a spooky tote bag, a Halloween afghan, or just adding a rebellious touch to your granny square collection, this pattern is a joy to work up.

## Materials Needed

  * Yarn of your choice (3 colors)
  * Crochet hook (a 3.00mm hook is used in the example)
  * Darning needle
  * Scissors



## Abbreviations Used

  * ch = chain stitch
  * dc = double crochet
  * hdc = half double crochet
  * dtr = double treble crochet
  * sc = single crochet
  * Sl St = slip stitch for joining to different round
  * ch-sp = chain space
  * st = stitch
  * sk = skip
  * inc = increase
  * tl = third loop



_This pattern uses USA crochet terminology_

## Tips & Color Suggestions

The completed granny square measures approximately 5.12 x 5.12 inches. Choosing the right colors can completely change the vibe of your square:

  * **Gothic Elegance:** Try a black skull, deep purple petals, and a charcoal border for dramatic flair.
  * **Soft & Subtle:** Consider a cream skull with dusty rose petals on a sage green background for a gentler approach.
  * **Halloween Spirit:** Orange skull with burgundy petals framed in chocolate brown is perfect for seasonal decorating with a sophisticated spooky style.

## Pattern Instructions

**Round 1:** Magic loop, ch3(count as dc),7dc ch5 dc ch3 dc ch5 dc in the loop, sl st to the top of ch3.

**Round 2:** ch3(count as dc), dc in next gap between 2 dc sts, dc inc in next 5 gaps, dc, 9sc in next ch-sp, sc+ch2+4dc+ch2+sc in next ch-sp, 9sc in next ch-sp, dc in next gap. Sl st to the top of the first ch3, fasten off.

**Round 3:** _Third loop(tl) only._ New yarn in the tl of the third dc inc, ch1 as a standing st, sk1, 5dc in the next tl, sk1, sl st, sl st, sk1, 5dc in next tl, sk1, sl st, [sl st, sk1, 5dc in next tl, ch2, sk5, 5dc in next tl, sk1, sl st] repeat 2 times, [sl st, sk1, 5dc in next tl, sk1, sl st] repeat 2 times, join to the first st.

**Round 4:** sl st in the same st, sc in next dc st, hdc inc, dc inc, hdc inc, sc, sl st to the top of next sl st, [sl st to the top of next sl st, sc, hdc inc, dc inc, hdc inc, sc, sl st to the top of next sl st] repeat 7 times, join to the first st, fasten off.

**Round 5:** _Third loop(tl) only._ New yarn in tl of the last sl st, ch3(count as dc), dc, ch3, sk3, sc, hdc, ch3, sk3, dtr, ch3, dtr, ch3, sk3, hdc, sc, ch3, [2dc, ch3, sk3, sc, hdc, ch3, sk3, dtr, ch3, dtr, ch3, sk3, hdc, sc, ch3] repeat 3 times, sl st to the top of the first ch3.

**Round 6:** ch3(count as dc), dc, 3dc in next ch-sp, 2dc, 3dc in next ch-sp, dc, 2dc+ch3+2dc in next ch-sp, dc, 3dc in next ch-sp, 2dc, 3dc in next ch-sp, [2dc, 3dc in next ch-sp, 2dc, 3dc in next ch-sp, dc, 2dc+ch3+2dc in next ch-sp, dc, 3dc in next ch-sp, 2dc, 3dc in next ch-sp] repeat 3 times, sl st to the top of the first ch3.

**Round 7:** ch1+sc, 11sc, 2sc+ch1+2sc in corner, 12sc, [12sc, 2sc+ch1+2sc in corner, 12sc] repeat 3 times, sl st to the first sc, fasten off.
