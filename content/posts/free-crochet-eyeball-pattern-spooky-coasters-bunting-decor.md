## Why You’ll Love This Crochet Eyeball Pattern

This pattern is worked flat in the round, making it an excellent project for confident beginners and intermediate crocheters alike. By using size 7 to 10 crochet cotton and a 2.5mm hook, you get tight, crisp stitches that really make the iris and veins pop.

 

Once finished, these eyeballs are incredibly versatile. You can use them as:

  * **Creepy Coasters:** The stiffened cotton makes them perfect for protecting your tables from cold brews or potion ingredients.
  * **Spooky Bunting:** String several together to create a garland that is sure to catch the eye. ## Materials and Supplies

To bring your crochet eyeball to life, gather the following supplies:

 

  * **Crochet Cotton Ball (Size 7 to 10):** You will need 3 colors—Iris color, Outer eye (white/sclera) color, and Edging color. 
    * _Note: Crochet thread is labelled with a number, like a 3 or a 10. This refers to the thickness of the thread—the smaller the number, the thicker the thread._
  * **Crochet Hook:** Size 2.5mm
  * **Scissors**
  * **Embroidery Needle:** Rounded point
  * **Embroidery Thread or Crochet Cotton:** In red (or your preferred vein color)
  * **Spray Starch:** For stiffening your finished eyeball (optional, but recommended for coasters)



 

## Abbreviations Used (US Version)

This pattern uses standard US crochet terminology:

 

  * **ch** – Chain
  * **ch st** – Chain stitch
  * **ch sp** – Chain space
  * **sc** – Single crochet
  * **dc** – Double crochet
  * **tr** – Treble or Triple crochet
  * **sl st** – Slip stitch

## The Crochet Eyeball Pattern

**Measurement:** Approximately 8 cm **Base:** Chain 4 and join with a slip stitch to create a loop.

 

**Row 1:** Ch 2, crochet 11 dc into loop. Join with sl st. Fasten off.

 

**Row 2:** Take your new colour (Outer eye). Sl st into any ch st, 2 ch, 1 dc into first ch st, 2 dc into the next 11 ch st. Sl st to join. _(24 stitches in total, incl 2 ch)_

 

**Row 3:** 2 ch-1 dc into the same ch sp, 2 dc into the next 3 ch st, 1 dc into the next 2 ch sp, 2 dc into the next 4 ch sp, 1 dc into the next 2 ch sp, 2 dc into the next 4 ch sp, 1 dc into the next 2 ch sp, 2 dc into the next 4 ch sp, 1 dc into the next 2 ch sp, sl st to join. _(40 stitches, incl 2 ch)_

 

**Row 4:** 3 ch, 1 tr into the next 39 ch sp, sl st to join.

 

![](https://craftedandcherished.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/Free-Crochet-Eyeball-Pattern-Step-2.webp)

 

 

**Row 5:** 3 ch, 1 tr into first ch sp, _1 tr into next ch sp, 2 tr into next ch sp_. Repeat from * to * 19 times. 1 tr into last ch sp, sl st to join. Fasten off. _(60 stitches incl ch)_

 

**Row 6:** Take your edging colour. Sl st into any ch sp, 2 ch, 1 dc into the same ch sp, _1 dc into next ch sp, 2 dc into next ch sp_. Repeat from * to * 19 times. 1 dc into last two ch sp.

## Finishing Off and Adding the Creepy Veins

The veins are what truly bring this crochet eyeball to life! Here is how to add them:

 

  1. **Prep your thread:** For this tutorial, I split my embroidery thread in two for thinner veins, and cut my thread into lengths of 40 cm. The amount of thread you use will depend on how many veins you want.
  2. **Stitching:** Using either embroidery thread or crochet cotton, thread your embroidery needle. Start in the red edging, coming from the back and threading through to the front.
  3. **Randomize:** Pick up crochet stitches in each row randomly. There is no strict order—create the veins as you go along! You can create thin, subtle veins as shown in this pattern, or thicker veins for a more bloodshot look.
  4. **Clean up:** Weave in all ends and cut off any excess thread.



 

**Pro-Tip for Coasters:** Spray starch onto your finished eyeball and block it to make it more stiff. This ensures your coaster lies flat and keeps its perfect circular shape!
