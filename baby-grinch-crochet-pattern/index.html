About This Baby Grinch Crochet Pattern

The Baby Grinch amigurumi is one of the most beloved holiday crochet toys of recent years. Crafters around the world have fallen head over heels for this design, and it’s easy to see why — the result is an irresistibly grumpy little character dressed in his signature Christmas outfit, small enough to tuck under a tree but detailed enough to become a treasured keepsake.

The finished Grinch stands 25 cm (11.8 inches) tall and features his iconic green fur, hand-sculpted muzzle, big expressive eyes, red Santa cap with a fluffy white pompom, and a candy-cane striped scarf. He’s worked primarily in Himalaya Dolphin Baby yarn — a plush, chenille-style yarn that gives him that irresistibly soft, velvety look.

🎄 Why crafters love this pattern: This baby Grinch crochet pattern is a perennial Christmas bestseller. The combination of plush green yarn, big 18mm safety eyes, and a hand-sculpted muzzle creates a toy that looks professionally made — yet is absolutely achievable for intermediate crocheters.

This is not a beginner-level pattern. You’ll need familiarity with magic rings (MR), single crochet (sc), slip stitches (sl st), increases (inc), decreases (dec), and basic muzzle sculpting. If you’re newer to amigurumi, build your skills first with our Amigurumi Corn on the Cob Crochet Pattern.

💡 Yarn Substitution Tip: Can’t find Himalaya Dolphin Baby? Any chenille or velvet-style yarn in a similar weight will work beautifully. The key is that plush, velvety texture that makes the Grinch look so soft and huggable. Avoid standard smooth acrylic — it won’t give the same effect.

Pro Tips Before You Begin

  • Crochet tightly. Amigurumi toys require tight, dense stitches so the stuffing doesn’t peek through. Go down a hook size if needed.
  • Use a stitch marker. Always mark the beginning of each round — chenille yarn makes it notoriously hard to count stitches visually.
  • Work in spirals. Don’t join rounds unless specified. If magic rings are tricky, start with ch 2 and work into the first chain.
  • Stuff gently and gradually. Overstuffing changes the shape. Add fiberfill a little at a time, especially for the head.
  • The tighter the crochet, the better it ages. A tightly crocheted toy keeps its shape and looks for much longer.
  • Pin before sewing. Always pin pieces in place before attaching permanently — especially important for the eyebrows and nose.
  • Size depends on your yarn. The 25 cm finished size assumes the exact recommended yarn. Thicker yarn will produce a larger Grinch.

Full Baby Grinch Crochet Pattern Instructions

This free crochet Grinch pattern is worked in pieces and assembled. We recommend completing all parts before joining. Below are the full stitch-by-stitch instructions.

🟢 Arms — Make 2 (Green yarn)

  • Rnd 16 sc into MR
  • Rnd 2(1 sc, inc) × 3 — 9 sts
  • Rnd 39 sc
  • Rnd 45 sl st, 4 sc — 9 sts
  • Rnds 5–139 sc for 9 rounds
  • FinishStuff ⅓ of arm, shape the bend. Flatten and crochet 4 sc through both loops. Fasten off and break yarn.

🟢 Legs — Make 2 (Green yarn)

  • Rnd 1Ch 6, work around chain: 4 sc, 3 sc in last ch, 4 sc, 3 sc in last ch, place marker — 14 sts
  • Rnd 24 sc, (inc) × 3, 4 sc, (inc) × 3 — 20 sts
  • Rnd 34 sc, (inc, 1 sc) × 3, 4 sc, (inc, 1 sc) × 3 — 26 sts
  • Rnd 426 sc
  • Rnd 5Sc to middle of heel, stop. Adjust marker to heel — 26 sts
  • Rnd 6(11 sc, dec) × 2 — 24 sts
  • Rnd 76 sc, (dec) × 6, 6 sc — 18 sts (all 6 decs at center toe)
  • Rnd 8(1 sc, dec) × 6 — 12 sts — stuff legs now
  • Rnds 9–1112 sc (3 rounds)
  • FinishComplete stuffing. Cut yarn on first leg and secure. Leave yarn on second leg to join.

🟢 Body (continued from legs, green yarn)

Joining the Legs: Join legs at the back. Chain 2 from the second leg and connect to the first leg. Toes face forward. Place stitch marker.

  • Rnd 1318 sc on leg 1, 2 sc on chain (front), 18 sc on leg 2, 2 sc on chain (back) — 40 sts
  • Rnds 14–1840 sc (5 rounds)
  • Rnd 19(18 sc, dec) × 2 — 38 sts
  • Rnd 20(17 sc, dec) × 2 — 36 sts
  • Rnd 21(4 sc, dec) × 6 — 30 sts
  • Rnds 22–2330 sc (2 rounds)
  • Rnd 24(3 sc, dec) × 6 — 24 sts
  • Rnd 25Attach arms: 8 sc gap on front/back between arms, crochet 4 sc with each arm as you go — 24 sts
  • Rnd 26(2 sc, dec) × 6 — 18 sts
  • Rnd 2718 sc

🟢 Head (continued from body, green yarn)

  • Rnd 29(3 sc, inc) × 6 — 30 sts — stuff neck
  • Rnd 30(4 sc, inc) × 6 — 36 sts
  • Rnd 31(5 sc, inc) × 6 — 42 sts
  • Rnd 32(6 sc, inc) × 6 — 48 sts
  • Rnds 33–3648 sc (4 rounds)
  • Rnd 37(6 sc, dec) × 6 — 42 sts
  • Rnd 38(5 sc, dec) × 6 — 36 sts
  • Rnds 39–4336 sc (5 rounds) — insert safety eyes between Rnds 38 & 39, 7 sc apart
  • Rnd 44(4 sc, dec) × 6 — 30 sts — stuff head firmly

⚠️ Head Stuffing Note: Stuff the head very firmly with fiberfill. The cheeks should have a pleasing, full roundness — this is what gives your Baby Grinch those adorable chubby cheeks!

🎨 Muzzle Sculpting & Design

Use double-strand light green YarnArt Jeans for sculpting. With a tapestry needle, insert the thread at a point parallel to the right eye at round 28, leaving a tail. Pull through to the top of the eye at half-eye height, grab the stuffing firmly, insert through the other side, and pull out 2 stitches away. Tighten and knot. Repeat on the other side to create the distinctive sculpted muzzle shape.

For the muzzle design, use 1-ply black YarnArt Jeans. Sew on the nose in the center under the eyes. Embroider a vertical stripe from the nose center (2 rounds long), then a smile with 3 stitches on each side. Embroider eyelids along the top arc of each eye and secure with glue if needed. Finish with black eyebrows and white eye highlights using white plush yarn.

Santa Cap & Striped Scarf Pattern

The iconic red-and-white Santa cap and candy-cane scarf are what make this baby Grinch crochet pattern unmistakably Christmas. Here’s how to crochet both pieces.

🎅 Cap (White pompom first, then Red)

Pompom (white): Work Rnds 1–6 in white, then switch to red for the rest of the cap.

  • Rnd 16 sc into MR
  • Rnd 2(inc) × 6 — 12 sts
  • Rnds 3–512 sc (3 rounds)
  • Rnd 6(dec) × 6 — 6 sts — stuff pompom
  • Rnd 7Switch to red. (1 sc, inc) × 3 — 9 sts. Cut white, secure.
  • Rnd 89 sc
  • Rnd 9(2 sc, inc) × 3 — 12 sts
  • Rnds 10–16Continue increasing by 3 sts every other round, alternating with plain rounds, up to 21 sts
  • Rnds 17–31Continue increases every other round up to Rnd 31: (13 sc, inc) × 3 — 45 sts
  • Rnds 32–3545 sc (4 rounds)
  • BrimSwitch to white. Crochet edge using HDC clusters: make 2 HDC together in each stitch all around. Fasten off.

🧣 Scarf (Red & White, back-and-forth rows)

  • Row 1Red. Ch 3, work 2 sc into chain, ch 1, turn.
  • Row 22 sc
  • Row 3Switch to white. 2 sl st. Do not cut red.
  • Row 42 sc
  • Row 5Switch to red. 2 sl st. Do not cut white.
  • Rows 6–8Continue alternating pattern
  • RepeatWork this 8-row sequence 8 times total = 72 rows. Fasten off. Glue ends securely.

Assembly & Finishing Touches

Once all pieces are crocheted, it’s time to bring your Baby Grinch to life. Follow these steps for the best results:

  • Pin all pieces in place before sewing — this ensures perfect symmetry.
  • Sew the head securely onto the neck, stuffing any remaining gaps as you go.
  • Embroider eyebrows straight across, marking their position with pins first so they’re even.
  • Add eye whites using white plush yarn, similar to the eyelid technique — lay yarn along the bottom of each eye and secure.
  • Position the Santa cap at a slight angle for extra character and tack it down with a few stitches or fabric glue.
  • Wrap the scarf around the neck and secure the ends with glue for a neat finish.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is there a baby Grinch crochet pattern free PDF available?

There are several Grinch-inspired amigurumi patterns shared freely within the crochet community, including on Ravelry. This article provides a comprehensive written guide to help you stitch along. Search for “Ravelry free crochet Grinch pattern” to explore community-shared options.

What skill level is required for this crochet Grinch pattern?

This is an intermediate-level pattern. You should be comfortable with magic rings, working in spirals, increases, decreases, and basic embroidery. Beginners may want to start with our simpler amigurumi patterns first to build confidence.

Is this a no-sew Grinch crochet pattern free design?

Not entirely — the pieces are crocheted separately and joined. However, the arms are attached directly into the body at Rnd 25, which reduces separate sewing. The scarf ends are glued rather than sewn, keeping finishing nice and simple.

Can I make a baby Grinch outfit crochet pattern for a human baby?

This pattern is for a 25 cm stuffed toy. For a baby outfit inspired by the Grinch, you’d need separate garment patterns. Check out our free crochet baby hat patterns — adapt them with green yarn for a cute Grinch-themed baby hat using the same how to make easy crochet baby hats techniques.

How long does this baby Grinch crochet pattern take to complete?

Most intermediate crocheters finish in 6–10 hours across a few sessions. The muzzle sculpting and facial embroidery take the most patience — but they’re exactly what brings his grumpy personality to life.

What yarn can I substitute for Himalaya Dolphin Baby?

Any plush chenille or velvet yarn in a similar weight (approx. DK/worsted) will work. The key is that fluffy, velvety texture. Avoid smooth acrylic — it won’t give the same irresistible softness.

How do I crochet baby hats to match the Grinch theme?

Head to our free crochet baby hat instructions and how to crochet baby hats patterns free guides. Simply swap the yarn to green Dolphin Baby for an adorable Grinch-themed baby beanie to pair with your toy!
